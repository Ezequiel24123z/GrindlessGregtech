# tools/assets

Generators for the pack's own textures. Everything is procedural 16x16 pixel art: each pixel is generated by code (palettes, text maps and a small shader). Nothing from GTM or other mods is copied or retouched. The style follows GregTech: light from the top left, shading with 3-5 tones per material, selective outlines on objects and no semi-transparent pixels.

## Regenerate

Run from the repo root. `-ExecutionPolicy Bypass` only affects that process (Windows PowerShell 5.1 blocks unsigned scripts by default).

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-SteamTextures.ps1
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-SteamTextures.ps1 -Only drill,keys
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-SteamTextures.ps1 -Credits
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-SteamPreview.ps1 -Out C:\path\steam-assets.png -Casing C:\path\casing_16x16.png
```

- `-Only` accepts `drill`, `greenhouse`, `bits`, `probe`, `sample` and `keys`.
- `-Credits` prints the rows of the "Assets" table in `CREDITS.md`.
- `-Casing` is optional and only gives the preview some context (any 16x16 PNG works, for example GTM's bronze casing extracted from the jar and kept outside the repo). Without it, a plain bronze background drawn by code is used.
- It can be run as many times as you like: the output is identical byte for byte. When it finishes, the script checks size, opaque or transparent pixels, no pure black, a grayscale core and that every emissive pixel also exists in the color layer.

## How to retouch

- Colors are at the top of each block (`New-Palette`), with one letter per color. The text maps (`$DrillBase`, `$GhBase`, `$CoreRows`) are edited by hand: `.` keeps the pixel, `_` erases it.
- The drill bits (items) and the window drill bit use the same shader (`Draw-Helix`): a cone or cylinder lit from the top left, with a helix. With several threads and a long pitch, slanted flutes appear; in the window the phase advances a quarter per frame so it spins.
- Every new file is noted in `CREDITS.md`, in the "Assets" table.

## LV era

Same method (`Pixel.Lib.ps1`, text maps, palettes and small shaders; nothing copied from GTM) with a different palette: **neutral** dark steel, like the grays of GTM's overlays on their warm casings, with small, saturated accents (red and green lamps, yellow and black hazard tape, and the orange of copper and of the tap stream).

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-LVTextures.ps1
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-LVTextures.ps1 -Only drill,models
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-LVTextures.ps1 -Credits
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\assets\Build-LVPreview.ps1 -Out C:\path\lv-assets.png -SteelCasing C:\path\machine_casing_solid_steel.png -HeatproofCasing C:\path\machine_casing_heatproof.png -LvHull C:\path\lv_side.png
```

- `-Only` accepts `drill`, `greenhouse`, `arc`, `simulator`, `electrode` and `models` (comma-separated, also with `-File`). `-Verbose` warns if a later drawing covers an emissive pixel.
- The preview accepts `-Section drill,greenhouse,arc,simulator,items,models` and `-Scale` to review a single piece at a larger size. The three casings are context only (any 16x16 PNG works, for example the GTM ones extracted from the jar and kept outside the repo); without them, backgrounds drawn by code are used.
- The final check is the steam one plus: no opaque pixel in the outer ring of the casing (1 px on the multiblocks, 2 px on the machine, which sits on the hull), and the model's core only paints where layer 0 has pixels.
- Output is identical byte for byte on every run.

### How to retouch (LV)

- The borders, the neutral steel ramp (`$SteelSpec`, letters `A`-`J` from light to dark) and each piece's palettes are at the top of its block. The text maps (`$DrillBase`, `$GhBase`, `$SafBase`, `$SimBase`, `$MobRows`) are edited by hand; what moves (drill bit, plants, bath, scan beam) is drawn by code frame by frame.
- The noise in the furnace bath comes from `Get-Noise` (a hash of position and frame), not from a random generator, so the output does not change.
- The same creature pixels serve the machine's hologram and the model's core (`$MobRows`): change the silhouette and both change.

#### How to retouch (IV foundation)

- Each piece's colours are at the top of its block (`New-Pal` fails on a repeated letter), with its map next to them: `$PelletMap`, `$TubeMap`, `$AssemblyMap`, `$SourceMap`, `$RtgMap`, `$BeadMap` with `$ResinBeads` (bead positions, back to front), `$ChartMap`, `$VenusCoreRows` and `$MercuryCoreRows`.
- Assemblies: `$AssemblyVariants` holds one entry per assembly (`Steel`, `Core`, `Band`, an optional `Blanket`, and `Outline`). Upper-case `A` to `E` are the core rods; lower-case `a` to `e` are the two outer rod rows at each end, which copy the core unless `Blanket` is set (as in the breeders). A new fuel is one more entry, and the check keeps the silhouette shared.
- Gauze: `$GauzeFold` is the crease (u + v); a lower value folds a bigger corner.
- Samples: `$VenusCapsule` and `$MercuryCapsule` hold the `Body` and `Seal` ramps (light to dark) and the glass highlights. Keep the cores light: the game multiplies them by the vein colour, and Venus's naquadah (`5A5A6E`) is dark.
- Brick: `$BrickPal`, `$BrickMottle`, `$BrickGradient`, `$BrickBiasAmp` and `$BrickPore`. Keep it pale and low contrast, because controller faces sit on it.
- Thin-film: `$FilmPal` (cells), `$FilmGlint` (the busbars' brighter column) and `$FramePal` (tungstensteel, a little darker than GTM's `6464A0`).

#### How to retouch (Venus)

- **Palettes and maps:** each face has its palette (`$DrillPal`, `$EnginePal`, `$WorksPal`) and its map (`$DrillBase`, `$EngineBase`, `$WorksBase`). `New-Pal` fails on a repeated letter. In a map, `.` keeps the casing and every letter is a colour. What moves is drawn by code over the map.
- **Drill:**
  - `$DrillRing` is the porthole's resting heat, by distance from its centre.
  - `$DrillPulse` is the porthole's breath, in steps dimmer.
  - `$DrillNeedle` holds the needle's tips, and `$DrillNeedleRun` the working sequence.
  - `$DrillThermoTop` is the hot column's top row per frame; `$DrillBulb` is the bulb.
- **Engine:**
  - `$EnginePins` and `$EngineArms` are the crank pin's eight places on the rim and the arm to each.
  - `$EnginePiston` is the displacer's row per frame, in step with the crank.
  - `$EngineSlotX` lists the grille's slots; `$EngineLamp` is the output lamp.
  - The two heat ramps (`$DrillHeatSpec`, `$EngineHeatSpec`) are separate, so either can change alone.
- **Acid works:**
  - `$WorksBubbles` holds the column and phase of each bubble.
  - The gauze tones are `t u v` when it glows and `p q s` when idle.
  - `$WorksLines` holds each line's pulse colour and phase.
  - `$WorksWaterLamp` and `$WorksOxygenLamp` are the two lamps.
- **Glow zones:** `$DrillGlowZone`, `$EngineGlowZone` and `$WorksGlowZone` say where light may come from. A new light outside them fails the check, so add it there as well.
