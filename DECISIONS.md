- 2026-10-24 · HV: syngas plant (`gtceu:syngas_plant`, type `gtceu:syngas`). It gasifies with steam, coal, coke, charcoal or powdered carbon, and the refinery's heavy fuel. It shifts CO with steam over an iron and chromium catalyst, and separates H₂, CO and CO₂, each gas to its own hatch. It also turns the excess CO in HV into H₂ (silicon, wood gas, Nether air). Recipes are based on real chemistry with GTM's units (steam counts as water, one mole per 1,000 mB; each recipe balances C, H and O), and the energy of the stages is summed. It does not reform methane (GTM already does that) and does not touch the reformer, which still runs on naphtha. CO₂ is inert in GTM: it can be discarded or used.
