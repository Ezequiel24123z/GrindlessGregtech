## What's new

**UXV: the galactic arm.** Twenty-one new multiblocks of ours build spin-based logic at home, go back to Cygnus X-1 to work its star and its disk, and sling out to two new places, the Vela pulsar and Cassiopeia A. They end at the gate to OpV. A new quest chapter (UXV - Spin and Strange Stars, 49 quests), a UXV Lines chart, the Power tab's UXV band and a UXV band in the multiblocks chapter come with it.

- Route: spintronics at home (MTJ sputter cluster, oxide PLD chamber, vdW stacking cell, racetrack fab, YIG LPE furnace) joined into GT--'s spintronic mainframe, the UXV circuit; the SHeQUID works makes the superfluid gyroscope for the UXV sensor; superheavies III (elements 121 and 122) and the MNT collider's island chart; at Cygnus X-1 the stellar elevator, plasma mass filter, stellar condensation tower, accretion disk skimmer and FRC former; the Kerr sling throws relay seeds to Vela and Cassiopeia A; at Vela the pasta extractor, phase sorter, draw bench and laminator make the UXV cable and hull plate; at Cassiopeia A the ejecta knot sweeper gathers titanium-44 for the annihilation laser works and the UXV emitter.
- New places (save-relevant, final ids): `grindless:vela_pulsar_orbit` (warp gate c8 from Cygnus X-1 orbit, needs an FRC cartridge) and `grindless:cas_a_remnant` (c9, needs a CNT Whipple shield). The first jump places a relay platform (ring, wireless energy, AE2 storage) as at UIV. Neither has sunlight.
- Power: the BZ dynamo at Cygnus X-1 (1,073,741,824 EU/t), the polar cap tap at Vela (536,870,912 EU/t) and the Mk6 stellar burning ring at home (`gtceu:uxv_fusion_reactor`: hot CNO, triple-alpha, carbon and oxygen burning, each fuel from the previous fuel's ash; 10,240,000,000 EU at 16 hatches). Wireless energy reaches UXV: UXV hatches (2/4/16/64 A), covers and the UXV SMES cell (4,026,531,840,000 EU).
- New UXV Parallel Control Hatch (x65,536) and the stellar fractionation hall (13x13x25, distillation tower and distillery, up to x65,536 in one hall).
- The gate to OpV: the Matrioshka computer charts the arm (circuit 4); the warp gate at Cygnus X-1 orbit holds 2^31 EU/t (16 A UXV) for 1,200 ticks (circuit 10, `hold_galactic_bulge`) and returns the bulge chart, which researches the OpV machine casing (assembly line).
- `highTierContent` is now on in `gtceu.yaml`: GTM registers the UHV-and-up components, basic machines, hulls and hatches (UHV to OpV). Every recipe, table, EMI page and quest uses GTM's `gtceu:{uev,uiv,uxv}_<component>` ids.
- Tier parity: UHV to UXV recipes take their own tier's components, circuits, hull and voltage, so many controller costs changed. Recipes built before a tier's hull stay one tier lower, because they make that tier's components. The droplet radiator module is now an assembly-line recipe in Mercury orbit, so the Droplet Radiator Works no longer needs a module to be built first.
- The Matrioshka computer controller now takes UIV pistons, robot arms and sensors; the Matrioshka node and TPV shell take UIV sensors and the emitter shell UIV pumps (they took field generators and emitters). All of them are buildable in Mercury orbit before the UIV jumps.
- Multiblock tooltips are short, GCYM-style (what it does, its recipe types, shared feature lines, at most one machine line); the details moved to each controller's EMI information page. All 259 controllers.

## What changes in your game

- Retired components: the 24 `grindless:{uev,uiv,uxv}_*` components (motor, pump, conveyor, piston, robot arm, field generator, emitter, sensor), the retired `grindless:uxv_*` parts among them, stay registered but hidden; each converts 1:1 to its `gtceu:` component in a crafting grid.
- The 15 UEV, UIV and UXV hulls and energy hatches (`gtceu:{uev,uiv,uxv}_{machine_hull,energy_input_hatch,energy_output_hatch,energy_input_hatch_16a,energy_output_hatch_16a}`) keep their ids and placed blocks; GTM registers them now. Tested: a v0.14.0 world keeps them as GTM machines.
- GCYR's space elevator now needs the UEV hull.
- Quests: the UEV and UIV chapters changed task ids where a task asked for a retired component. Progress on those tasks starts again; claimed rewards stay.
- Item slots: the warp gate's input slots go from 6 to 7 and the Matrioshka computer's from 3 to 5.
- GT--'s neutronium mixture and plutonium fuel are hidden for good with their recipes (no source).
- The OpV hull and energy hatches are visible in the recipe viewer (GTM's ids, for OpV).
- New shapes only for new machines: no older multiblock changes shape. Recipe costs of UHV-UXV controllers changed (tier parity); machines already built are not affected.
- Nothing is removed. New dimension ids are final.

## Known risks

- Multiblock previews in EMI show the first block each part slot accepts, and GTM keeps those candidates in a hash set, so their order can change with any startup change. When a hidden part comes first, EMI drops that preview. This release hit it with Extended Features' cleaning maintenance hatch (50 previews); that hatch is now hidden from the item list only, so some previews show it in the maintenance slot (any maintenance hatch works). Later EMI audits watch the previews check for it.
- The UEV parallel hatch is untested in a GCyM large machine, and no relay platform was placed in the save runs.
