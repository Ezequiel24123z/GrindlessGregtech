UXV adds twenty-one multiblocks that build spin-based logic at home, work Cygnus X-1's star and disk, and reach the Vela pulsar and Cassiopeia A. They end at the gate to OpV.

- New lines: the spintronic mainframe circuit, superheavies III, Cygnus X-1's stellar elevator and disk skimmer, Vela's pulsar pasta and Cassiopeia A's titanium-44.
- Power: the BZ dynamo, the polar cap tap and the Mk6 stellar burning ring. Wireless energy, a UXV SMES cell and a UXV Parallel Control Hatch (x65,536) reach UXV.
- UHV to UXV recipes take their own tier's parts, so controller costs changed. All 259 controllers have short tooltips, with details on each controller's EMI page.
- New UXV Spin and Strange Stars quest chapter and a UXV Lines chart.
- Warp gate input slots go from 6 to 7, the Matrioshka computer's from 3 to 5, and GCYR's space elevator now needs the UEV hull.

Upgrading: the retired grindless UEV, UIV and UXV components stay hidden and convert 1:1 to GTM's components in a crafting grid. Progress on UEV and UIV quest tasks that asked for one starts again; claimed rewards stay.
