UHV takes you to Proxima Centauri b, builds a Dyson swarm around the Sun and opens a warp gate to Proxima.

- A level 8 rocket with the new ICAN rocket motor and the BNNT fuel tank reaches Proxima b; GTCA's space elevator now has recipes.
- The warp gate is a 15x15x3 ring in Earth orbit and in Proxima b's orbit. While both run the 16 A UHV sustain, a player steps out of the other ring.
- Dyson swarm machines (swarm segment foundry, swarm control station, solar probe bay) and Proxima machines (xenoflora rover bay, sample receiving facility, cold-trap drill, biomining heap).
- At home: transuranium facility, MBE reactor, photonic foundry, SiC device fab, positron accumulator, Casimir array and ZBLAN draw tower.
- Wireless energy reaches UHV with a UHV SMES cell (24 UV naquadria batteries); Proxima b orbit and Mercury orbit are station slots 7 and 8.
- The Horizon Collector, an LV quest gift, turns a circuit into basic start materials.
- New UHV Age quest chapter, UHV processing-lines chart and updated Power tab.
