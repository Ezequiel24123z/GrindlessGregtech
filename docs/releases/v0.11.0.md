## What's new

**UHV: Proxima and the Swarm.** New multiblocks of ours take you to Proxima Centauri b, build a Dyson swarm around the Sun, and open a warp gate between Earth orbit and Proxima. The space elevator from GT Community Additions finally gets its recipes. A new quest chapter (UHV Age), a UHV processing-lines chart and an updated Power tab come with it.

- **Getting there:** a level 8 rocket, using the new ICAN rocket motor (a GCYR rocket motor of tier 6) and the BNNT fuel tank, reaches Proxima b. The motor is built from a GEO-assembled stage lifted by the space elevator. Its UHTC tile and nozzle come from the new spark plasma sinterer, its zirconia from GTCA red zircon in the EBF; a tantalum carbide liner lines it.
- **Heavy elements (transcurium):** HFIR driver elements and targets, curium, berkelium, californium (the Cf-252 neutron source) and einsteinium, in the transuranium facility.
- **Crystals and clocks:** the thorium-229 nuclear clock, lithium niobate, Eu:YSO and InP crystals and wafers.
- **Betavoltaics:** nickel-63 betavoltaic cells, the C-12 diamond spreader.
- **Space elevator:** GTCA's space elevator and its modules (tether ribbon and climber, GEO lift).
- **Photonics:** the MBE reactor and photonic foundry, with quantum cascade lasers, thin-film lithium niobate modulators, phase-change photonic dies, quantum memory chips and GT--'s optical processor.
- **Power devices:** the SiC device fab and the solar array fab (inverted metamorphic and red-tuned solar arrays).
- **Antimatter and Casimir:** the positron accumulator (sodium-22 sources) and the Casimir array (labelled fiction in EMI).
- **ZBLAN:** the ZBLAN draw tower draws fluoride fibre in Earth orbit.
- **Wireless energy reaches UHV:** UHV hatches, covers and the UHV SMES cell (24 UV naquadria batteries), made by the Naquadria Magnet Works. Tested on the bench: a 33,554,432 EU/t UHV base ran for 60 s on a network fed 43,514,928 EU/t (the UV mix plus four swarm control stations), with 41,339,182 EU/t reaching the bank.
- **Travel and stations:** Proxima b orbit and Mercury orbit as station slots 7-8; the Hermean quantum seed is made in Mercury orbit with a swarm segment as catalyst; the lunar solar field gains a Proxima row (red-tuned arrays, star-side only).
- **Mercury:** the regolith excavator digs Mercury regolith; the solar vapour furnace (daylight only) splits it into silicon monoxide, alumina-calcia residue and metals; MgB2 superconducting wire by powder-in-tube.
- **Dyson swarm:** the swarm segment foundry builds and refurbishes segments, the swarm control station turns them into power (up to 4x UHV amperage with the coronal field map), burning oganesson propellant pods; the solar probe bay maps the corona.
- **Proxima life:** the xenoflora rover bay brings back Proxima samples, the sample receiving facility (on Earth) isolates them, and the xenobotany greenhouse, xenobiochem plant and radiotrophic vat turn them into melanin composites, flare shield panels and engineered cultures; the biofoundry gains a far-red gene cassette.
- **Wetware:** the organoid array (on Earth) grows organoid processors from stem cells and turns them into wetware mainframes; its spent medium is bacterial sludge for the biofoundry. The orbital biology lab gains a UHV sterilised growth medium row.
- **Proxima ground:** the cold-trap drill cores deuterium-rich ice on Proxima b's night side, and the biomining heap leaches its basalt with three Proxima cultures (biocement, urea). Older machines gain Proxima rows: the heavy-water plant (circuit 3, four times the deuterium of Martian ice), the platinum plant, the rare-earth cascade, the regolith cell (leached tailings) and the tholin reformer in Saturn orbit.
- **The warp gate:** a 15x15x3 ring built twice, in Earth orbit and in Proxima b's orbit. Opening it with exotic matter and antihydrogen cartridges gives the Proxima gate seed that pairs AE2 between Earth and Proxima; while both ends run the 16 A UHV sustain, a player inside one ring steps out of the other. The baseline astrometer in Proxima b's orbit charts the stellar neighbourhood (ZBLAN fibre, infrared pigment), the research of the UEV machine casing.

**Early game: the Horizon Collector.** A quest gift at LV (with two solar covers): an uncraftable single-block machine that turns a circuit into basic start materials at 16 EU/t, slower than the steam drill and greenhouse.

## What changes in your game

- The betavoltaic fab's controller recipe uses a new pattern (it clashed with the transuranium facility's).
- Nothing is removed. No older multiblock changes shape, and existing saves keep every item, block and fluid.
- Tested on the bench: a world created with v0.10.0 opens with v0.11.0 and keeps its blocks, chest contents, UV energy tower (formed again, back on the wireless network) and saved data; all eight station orbits load, Mercury's and Proxima b's included.
- The ZBLAN draw tower, the cryogenic plant's Triton row and the MOCVD recipes lose a doubled path in their recipe ids (for example grindless:mocvd/mocvd/... is now grindless:mocvd/...); saved machines keep running, as GTM stores a running recipe in full, not by id.
- Some UHV items are shown in the recipe viewer for the first time; items from later eras stay hidden.
- Fixed: on Linux and macOS (dedicated servers, Mac clients) the pack's scripts could load in a different order than on Windows; every script now has a fixed load order.

## For contributors

- The test bench runs on a Linux VM first (3 parallel runs, client tests included) and falls back once to the home PC; T0 also runs in GitHub Actions. See `docs/testing/`.
- The reachability validator covers UHV (`tools\analysis\Invoke-Reachability.ps1 -Tier uhv`).
- Every KubeJS script starts with a stamped `// priority:` line derived from `tools/analysis/script_order.json`; never edit it by hand (`Update-PackIndex.ps1` stamps new scripts).
