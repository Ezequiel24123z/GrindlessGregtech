Long production lines from Steam to HV, built on real processes, and a first global audit before the release.

- Steel is made in three steps in the Steam Blast Furnace: iron sinter, pig iron, then Bessemer steel. (#244)
- Photographic plates use a real silver halide emulsion: nitric acid from coke-oven ammonia, silver nitrate, silver chloride, then the emulsion. (#245)
- Platinum is dissolved in aqua regia, and an MV chemical reactor can precipitate it. At HV the platinum slag gives rhodium and ruthenium, and the large chemical reactor's nitric acid runs on a platinum-rhodium gauze. (#246, #249)
- Every silicon boule takes polysilicon from the Siemens process: trichlorosilane, distillation and hydrogen reduction. (#247)
- PVC goes through 1,2-dichloroethane and its cracking, and PTFE through R22 and its pyrolysis. GTM's phenol, epoxy, vinyl chloride and TFE shortcuts are gone. (#248)
- Rocket fuel is Aerozine 50 with N₂O₄. Hydrogen peroxide comes from the anthraquinone cycle in GT--'s chemical plant, which needs HV machine casings, and UDMH from dimethylamine. (#250)
- Titanium tetrachloride is distilled from a crude, magnesium chloride is electrolysed back into magnesium and chlorine, and the regolith cell's titanium mode follows the same line. (#251, #268)
- Cerium, lanthanum and yttrium now start at EV; neodymium's native ore stays at HV. (#267)
- Charts and quests teach every step of the new lines from Steam to HV. (#252, #253, #258, #259, #273)
- Composed plants (the platinum plant, the mega chemical complex and the others) follow every recipe change. (#257)
- The pristine gas works runs up to 64 recipes at once. (#241)
- Relay platforms use the travelling player's team. (#255)
- Quests from EV to MAX+ follow the recipe order, teach the new wafers and machines, and give XP instead of production-line parts. (#256, #263, #264, #270)
- The README explains how to run a server. (#265)

Upgrading: worlds from v1.3.0 carry over. Machines set up for the old steel, plate, silicon, polymer, rocket fuel, titanium or rare earth recipes need their new inputs, and GT--'s chemical plant needs HV machine casings for peroxide and hydrazine.
