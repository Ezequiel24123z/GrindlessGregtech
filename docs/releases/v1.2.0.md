Fixes what ten audits of v1.1.0 found, about 180 problems (#196 to #209).

- Relay places can be reached again from UIV on: a finished jump places its platform and gives Relay Coordinates. (#203)
- The MAX+ chapter can be finished: two MAX quests asked for more shell segments than an inventory holds. (#215)
- Quest texts follow the recipes and the real crafting order. (#206, #216, #222)
- Mainframes from UIV to MAX need circuits of the tier below, and MAX+ hardware takes any MAX+ circuit. (#211)
- Costs from UHV to MAX climb with each step, and placeholder recipes that made UEV items from lead and iron are gone. (#217, #218, #226)
- Six machines gained a hatch their recipes need, and every machine that promises a parallel hatch takes one. (#210)
- Electric water-to-steam heaters are gone, and wireless energy stays exact above 2^53 EU. (#205, #217, #224)
- Smaller fixes: AE2 bridges work at the last six places, the Horizon Shard can be made at MAX, and recipe notes show text instead of raw keys. (#212, #223)
- Breaking a working Mirror Shell no longer crashes the server: the coils around its windows are now feeder coil casings.

Upgrading: a Mirror Shell built in v1.1.0 stays unformed until the coils that ring its windows are swapped for feeder coil casings (see EMI's multiblock page).
