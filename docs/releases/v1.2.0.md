## What's new

This release fixes what a full audit of v1.1.0 found. Ten independent reviews (two model families, every area of the pack) filed about 180 findings; this version fixes the serious ones and most of the rest.

**The pack can be finished from UIV on.** Since an earlier rework, no player could ever reach a relay place: the UIV platforms were never placed, and no far ring could hold the gate open. Now a completed jump places its platform once per world and puts Relay Coordinates (a data stick) in the jump ring's output bus. Put them in the input bus of a ring that is holding the gate open, step in, and you arrive. At the platform, step out and back in: its ring wakes for one hold, paid by its stocked cells and your wireless network, and carries you home. This works for all 13 relay places, Sgr A* and the four MAX+ places included.

**The MAX+ chapter can be completed.** Two MAX quests asked for more shell segments than an inventory holds, which locked The Horizon Lock and every MAX+ quest after it. All task counts now fit an inventory.

**Quests say what the pack does.** Every chapter was checked against the recipes: hundreds of texts fixed (ingredients, machines, places, the wireless rule, circuit ladders), quests ordered by the real crafting order, missing lessons added (new wafers, components, hulls), and rewards that handed out a current-era part replaced by XP.

**Circuits climb every tier.** Each mainframe from UIV to MAX now needs circuits of the tier below and runs at its own tier's voltage, so the OpV and MAX circuits are no longer cheaper than a UXV one. MAX+ hardware takes any MAX+ circuit (a new tier tag).

**Costs climb as the design says.** Amperage, hatches, controllers and components from UHV to MAX each cost clearly more than the step below; GregTech's placeholder recipes that made UEV and later items from lead and iron are gone. Machine casings from MV to UHV are made in the assembler only.

**Multiblocks run what they advertise.** Six machines lacked a hatch their own recipes need (two of them blocked UIV and UXV); every machine that promises a parallel hatch takes one; recipe pages show every input, the programmed circuit included. Breaking a working Mirror Shell no longer risks a server crash: its 1,528 superconducting coils (GregTech relights such blocks one nested check at a time) are now feeder coil casings.

**Power and the wireless network.** The electric water-to-steam heaters are gone (with the boosted turbine they made energy from nothing). The wireless network keeps energy exact above 2^53 EU, shares a shortage fairly, respects chunk claims and spreads its work over ticks.

**Smaller fixes.** AE2 bridges work at the last six places; the Horizon Shard can be made at MAX, so the ending cannot be locked by losing the Welcome quest's shards; the Steam and LV drill bits have machine recipes; recipe notes that showed raw keys have text; pages no longer claim places a recipe does not use; four pairs of items with the same name are told apart.

## What changes in your game

A v1.1.0 world keeps working (no item, block, fluid or quest id was removed or renamed). Some recipes from UHV on cost more, a few hand recipes moved to machines, and a few quests have new tasks or order. Platforms of relay places appear the next time a jump completes. A Mirror Shell built in v1.1.0 stays unformed until the superconducting coils that ring its windows are swapped for feeder coil casings (EMI's multiblock page shows where).

## For contributors

- Green means green: strict native summaries for batch and release runs, T0 runs every unit test and the strict quest lint, baselines only shrink, and channels move only after tools/release/Test-Promotion.ps1 passes.
- The reachability validator fails closed on unmodelled conditions, checks machine I/O, and proves MAX+.

- Every recipe handler registers through `global.grindless.recipes` (lib/recipes_guard.js) or holds a try block; every script keeps its declarations inside a function scope; the MAX+ gate's run state and the lunar quantum links persist as NBT tags.
- Fixtures that build a wireless tower take turns on `ggTestWirelessQueue`; with no player online the bench's force-loaded chunks do not tick entities, so fixtures must not wait on an explosion or a dropped item to move.

## Known risks

- Seven cost steps still climb less than x1.3: the machine casing at HV and UV, the hull at MV, HV, EV and UV, and the 1 A hatch at HV. Those casings are made of plates that cost less than the tier below's; the fix (each casing takes the one below it) waits for the owner's decision.
- To clear layout errors, about 27 dependency arrows in the UEV and UIV chapters were dropped (the quest order is unchanged); a layout pass will put them back.
- With more than about 400 wireless entries, one settlement spans several ticks, so the status readout can lag by that long.
- Explosion rituals (AE2 pairs) are proven by AE2's transform call on the bench, not by a live explosion, because the bench has no player to tick entities.
- GregTech's recycling yields of about 700 crafted items can differ between server starts (GregTech picks one of an item's recipes); each yield matches a real recipe.
- Design questions waiting for the owner are listed in issue #230: principle 7 after UV, the Hyperstargate against the 10x GTNH target, MAX+ power density, multiplayer wireless and travel, the pack licence and the casing chain.
