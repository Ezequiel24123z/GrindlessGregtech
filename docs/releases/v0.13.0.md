## What's new

**Quality review.** The whole pack, Steam to UEV, went through a quality pass after the owner's review: multiblocks that were plain boxes of one block are rebuilt to show their process, controllers above the early game are no longer made on the crafting table, every multiblock of ours explains itself in EMI, and working machines make a sound.

- **EMI works again for every GregTech process.** One structure preview aborted GTM's whole EMI integration, which hid every GT recipe category and workstation. Fixed; the test bench now fails if any GT display, workstation, structure preview or required info page goes missing.
- **Info pages:** each multiblock of ours has its own EMI info page (what it does, its modes, what it needs, where byproducts go), in English and Spanish.
- **Working sounds:** 52 recipe types of ours get a working-sound loop (generated audio), Steam to UEV. Machines that share a type share the loop; GTM's own types keep GTM's sound.
- **Horizon Collector:** one run every 8 s instead of 60 s, up to 4 output stacks per run, flint on circuit 10; still 16 EU/t.
- **Expanded Assembly Line (GTM Extended Features):** craftable at LuV in the assembly line, with its research path (LuV wireless data receptor, a transmissor in a Matrix Data Relay with a data access hatch).
- **Controller recipes:** Steam to HV controllers take one part of their own line (a drill bit, electrode or catalyst); EV controllers are made in the assembler with a fluid and two parts of their line.
- **Honest processes:** the culture seed no longer doubles from nothing (replication now eats agar); UV wafer, grating and structure-block steps get a real second ingredient or fluid.

## For contributors

- `recipe-cycles.mjs` (T0) fails if any controller cannot be built from what exists before it (no baseline).
- The reachability model now synthesises GTM's generated research recipes (scanner or research station + item + data item), so a machine researched with something only it unlocks fails its tier gate; site targets take `exceptRecipes`. Releases run the gate and eras passes for every implemented tier on the release dump.
- The Multiblocks quest generator skips controllers after the implemented era, gates up to that era, and recognises our controllers registered through `name:`/`lang:` tables.
- `recipe-cycles` now tracks the foreign ids (GTM materials, optical circuits, circuit tags) that only our recipes lead to, from `tools/analysis/recipe_bridge.json` (regenerate once per release with `--bridge-from <obtain dump> --write`), and a new `era-order` check fails a machine recipe that takes an item registered in a later era. Both run in T0.
- `test/recipe-twins.test.mjs` (T0) fails when two recipes of one type share an ingredient set (GTM's lookup would shadow one).
- Four static quality gates with baseline ratchets run in T0: `structure-quality.mjs`, `recipe-quality.mjs`, `process-audit.mjs`, `emi-coverage.mjs`; new findings fail. Standards: `docs/quality/{structures,recipes,items,emi,sounds}.md`.
