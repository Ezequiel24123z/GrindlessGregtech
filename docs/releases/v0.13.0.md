A quality pass over the whole pack, Steam to UEV: multiblocks that were plain boxes now show their process, controllers above the early game are no longer crafted by hand, every multiblock of ours explains itself in EMI, and working machines make a sound.

- EMI works again for every GregTech process: one structure preview had aborted GTM's whole EMI integration and hidden every GT recipe category and workstation.
- Each multiblock of ours has its own EMI info page (in English and Spanish): what it does, its modes, what it needs and where byproducts go.
- 52 recipe types of ours get a working-sound loop, Steam to UEV.
- Horizon Collector: one run every 8 s instead of 60 s, up to 4 output stacks per run, flint on circuit 10, still 16 EU/t.
- The Expanded Assembly Line (GTM Extended Features) is craftable at LuV in the assembly line, with its research path.
- Steam to HV controllers take one part of their own line, EV controllers are made in the assembler, and higher controllers moved to the assembler or assembly line, so their recipe ids changed.
- The culture seed no longer doubles from nothing (replication now eats agar), and UV wafer, grating and structure-block steps get a real second ingredient or fluid.
- Controllers that needed something only they or a later era could make now take other inputs; the engine test stand (MV) and composite autoclave (HV) come before the first rocket again.

Upgrading: many multiblocks changed shape and an old one will not form, so rebuild them (see the list below). Break and place again any Horizon Collector built before this version.
