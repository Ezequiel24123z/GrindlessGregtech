ZPM adds 27 multiblocks that take you from the home factory to Saturn's rings and moons and on to Uranus, with a bigger fusion reactor, new fuels and ZPM wireless energy.

- Saturn and Uranus each get their own orbit and station slot, and both stay dry. (#16)
- At home, the ZPM multiblocks cover naquadria, europium, the actinides, the magnet works, fusion, outer chemistry, qubits and the observatory. (#8 to #15)
- Saturn has the siphon circuits, the Titan probes and the science lines; Uranus has the arrival run, its orbit and its site lines. (#17 to #20)
- Fusion Reactor Mk2 and the D + He-3 fuel. (#13)
- Wireless energy reaches ZPM: hatches (including a 16 A wireless energy hatch), covers and the ZPM energy cell. (#11)
- New ZPM quest chapter, and the Power tab now covers ZPM. (#22)
- Proxima Centauri b moves from rocket tier 5 to tier 8; Saturn is tier 5 and Uranus tier 6.
- Wireless energy hatches now respect their rated amperage.
- The naquadria containment casing takes 2 naquadah alloy plates instead of naquadria dust.
