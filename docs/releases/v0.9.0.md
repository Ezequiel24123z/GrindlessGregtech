## What's new

**ZPM: Saturn and Uranus.** Twenty-seven new multiblocks of ours take you from the home factory to Saturn's rings and moons and on to Uranus, with a bigger fusion reactor, new fuels and the ZPM tier of the wireless network. A new quest chapter and an updated Power tab come with it.

- **Getting there:** Saturn and Uranus each get their own orbit and station slot. Both planets stay dry, like the other gas giants.
- **At home:** the ZPM multiblocks cover naquadria, europium, the actinides, the magnet works, fusion, outer chemistry, qubits and the observatory.
- **Saturn:** the siphon circuits, the titan probes and the Saturn science lines.
- **Uranus:** the arrival run, its orbit and the Uranus site lines.
- **Fusion:** the Fusion Reactor **Mk2** and the **D + He-3** fuel.
- **Wireless energy reaches ZPM:** ZPM hatches (including the 16 A wireless energy hatch), covers and the ZPM energy cell. The network now runs at ZPM.
- **Quest book:** a new chapter, **ZPM**, and the **Power** tab now covers ZPM.

## What changes in your game

- **Proxima Centauri b moves from rocket tier 5 to tier 8.** Saturn is tier 5 and Uranus tier 6.
- **Wireless energy hatches now respect their amperage.** Each hatch moves at most its rated amperage; before, a hatch could hand a power substation more than its rating.
- **The naquadria containment casing** takes 2 naquadah alloy plates instead of naquadria dust.
- GTCA's space elevator and its modules stay hidden until UV; they have no recipe yet.
- Nothing is removed. No older multiblock changes shape, and existing saves keep every item, block and fluid.
- Some ZPM items are shown in the recipe viewer for the first time; items from later eras stay hidden.
