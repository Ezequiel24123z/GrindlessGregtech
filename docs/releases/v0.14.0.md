## What's new

**UIV: dead stars.** New multiblocks of ours take you to a neutron star (RX J1856.5-3754), a pulsar (PSR J0437-4715) and a black hole (Cygnus X-1), and open the gate to UXV. A new quest chapter (UIV - Dead Stars, 54 quests), a UIV Lines chart, the Power tab's UIV band and 24 UIV multiblock quests come with it.

- Wireless energy now reaches UIV: UIV hatches (2/4/16/64 A) and covers, and the UIV SMES cell (nitride magnet works, circuit 4). Existing tiers unchanged.
- New UIV Insane Parallel Control Hatch (x16,384; UIV hull, sensor, emitter, UIV circuit, metallic hydrogen cable). It fits every multiblock that takes a parallel hatch; the UHV and UEV hatches are unchanged.
- New isotopes from the UEV calutron: titanium-50, europium-151 and nickel-58. Element 120 from a titanium-50 beam on a californium-249 wheel (cyclotron and recoil separator); neutron supermirrors and guides. The europium reductor gains a terbium reduction and gadolinium, erbium and praseodymium oxide cuts.
- New ramp-compression pulser (UIV): metallic hydrogen (UIV cable and wire), lonsdaleite (bearings for the UIV piston) and cubic gauche nitrogen. Their ambient-pressure quench is named fiction. Para hydrogen from the cryogenic plant, a solid para-H2 target frozen in superfluid helium, a protium draw-off on the heavy water plant, a metallic hydrogen winding in the magnet works and shock-grade pyrolytic graphite in the methane pyrolyser. GTM's metallic hydrogen cable and wire recipes are replaced.
- New levitation melter (UIV, 7x7x9): casts what no crucible holds. RHEA (W-Mo-Nb-Ta-Hf, the UIV hull plate), the Wf/RHEA first-wall billet, titanium-50, a Zr-Ti-Cu-Ni-Be metallic glass and PrNi5. Beryllium oxide fume is captured and electrolysed back; the oxide dross goes to the extraction cascade (new circuits 12-13: niobium-tantalum split and dross digestion). Also: an Nb-Ta electron-beam button, Halbach arrays from the nitride magnet works, densified tungsten preforms in the CVI furnace, RHEA slabs on the hot-rolling mill and the parts of the UIV conveyor.
- New TFLN fab and femtosecond writer (UIV): thin-film lithium niobate on silicon nitride, silicon-28 SOI wafers, periodically poled waveguides and an electro-optic microcomb; laser-written glass interposers, two-photon lattices, micro-orifice plates and 5D data crystals. The photonic foundry gains circuits 8-11, the synchrotron a damascene trench row (circuit 7) and the fibre-draw tower a silica storage preform (circuit 9).
- New BEUV scanner and STM lithography cell (UIV): 6.7 nm lithography with a terbium droplet source and La/B4C mirrors that foul and are cleaned, the BEUV driver ASIC, and phosphorus donors placed atom by atom in silicon-28. New gas: phosphine (scrubbed to phosphoric acid). The MBE reactor gains circuits 5-6, the photonic foundry circuit 12, the zone refiner circuits 6-7 (silicon-28 float-zone ingot and a lossy donor regrow), the spark plasma sinterer circuit 5 (B4C target) and the ZPM ion implanter circuit 6.
- New spectral hole burner and millikelvin cryostat works (UIV): six-hour and telecom memories from europium-151 and erbium YSO crystals (crystal furnace circuits 8-9), nuclear demagnetisation stages, millikelvin cryostat modules and the P:Si processor; erbium fibre and pumped EDFAs (fibre-draw tower circuits 10-11), praseodymium metal in the EBF and a He-3/He-4 mixture.
- The metallic hydrogen coil block now has an EMI information page.
- UIV optical mainframe: GT--'s optical mainframe now comes from the photonic integration hall (circuit 5), which also makes the photonic tensor core (circuit 4) and the pulsar-timed repeater (circuit 6). The UIV sensor is craftable. New: PCM crossbars (photonic foundry circuit 13), thorium-229 tetrafluoride film (fluorspar kiln, MBE circuit 7), a chip-scale nuclear clock (nuclear clock circuit 3) disciplined at the pulsar timing station (circuit 3). GT--'s mainframe recipe is replaced.
- UIV electric pump, robot arm and motor are craftable: a printed, HIPed and balanced RHEA impeller, a strain-wave gear with a suction-cast metallic glass flexspline, and a field-cooled GdBCO bulk magnet (Gd-123 precursor, seeded growth, oxygenation, impregnation). Kroll zirconium sponge is distilled in the EBF; RHEA swarf and powder and metallic glass gates return to the levitation melter (circuits 6-8).
- UIV Mk5 fusion: a new UIV fusion ring (gtceu:uiv_fusion_reactor, 5.12 G EU buffer) burns helium-3/helium-3, polarised Li-6 hydride and polarised 15N ammonia fuels; ignition energies exceed 2^31 EU (pn15 needs all 16 hatches full). New polarised proton source multiblock (proton polarisation, circuits 1-3), first-wall tiles, a printed tungsten mesh and a liquid Li-Sn divertor. The ash yields carbon-12.
- UIV Matrioshka: new Matrioshka computer (11x9x11) and droplet radiator works (9x5x13), both run in Mercury orbit. New lines: InGaAsSb thermophotovoltaic tiles behind a tungsten photonic-crystal emitter, Nb Josephson-junction AQFP logic, carbon-12 diamond plates (C-12 methanation in the HV syngas plant, circuit 7) and tin droplet radiators; the rad-hard controller is craftable. The neutron-star EOS and Kerr charts arrive with the UIV pulsar and Cygnus X-1 packages.
- UIV travel (save-relevant): three new tier-11 places, grindless:rx_j1856_orbit, grindless:psr_j0437_orbit and grindless:cygnus_x1_orbit. Build a relay seed (starchip fab circuit 3), throw it from the Moon with the laser sail launcher (circuit 2), then jump with the warp gate at Sirius B orbit (circuits 4-6); the first jump places a relay platform (ring, wireless energy, AE2 storage) at the far star. The Cygnus X-1 jump also needs a pulsar navigation solution and a neutronium pellet. New: the ergosphere probe (starchip fab circuit 4).
- UIV RX J1856.5-3754: new machines at the neutron star's orbit: the crust lift, the neutron drip separator, the neutronium cage, the r-process decompressor and the decay heat vault (a generator: 33,554,432 EU/t for 12,000 ticks from r-process decay heat). New: the neutronium pellet and the neutron-star site casing (assembler); the UIV field generator is craftable. Thirteen older machines gain rows. The crust lift and neutronium are named fiction.
- UIV PSR J0437-4715: new machines at the pulsar's orbit: the pulsar wind trap (pair plasma), the positronium condensate lab (positronium in aerogel, laser cooling, the gamma laser cavity) and the X-ray pulse profiler (the pulsar navigation solution for the Cygnus X-1 jump). The UIV emitter is craftable, which unlocks the UIV wireless dynamo hatches and covers and the UIV parallel hatch.
- UIV Cygnus X-1: new machines at the black hole's orbit: the ergosphere probe bay (9x7x9; probe runs, Kerr probe data, split and refitted sleds, sled hulls) and the Penrose engine (13x9x13 generator: a split charge sled dropped into the ergosphere comes back as a half-sled, 268,435,456 EU/t, 8 A UIV, for 2,400 ticks, about 20.7 % of the dropped mass-energy; it needs a 16 A UIV dynamo or two 4 A wireless dynamos). Five older machines gain rows: synroc liner (actinides), waste canister fill (hot cell), canister compaction (megabar press), the Kerr ringdown record (Ganymede) and correlated Kerr data (event horizon imaging). New items only, nothing removed.
- The gate to UXV: the warp gate's hold row now reaches Cygnus X-1 (circuit 7, 16 A UIV). The UXV machine casing gets an assembly-line recipe (research: Kerr chart).
- New UIV multiblock: Neutronium Compression Hall (compressor and implosion in one; neutronium pellets in the input bus set the parallels (4^pellets: 1 pellet 4, 4 pellets 256); the pellets are not consumed). Switch the hall's recipe type to implosion in its UI; implosion still needs its explosive. New shape (13x10x11).
- Greenhouses: the MV and HV fertilizer + CO2 mode now uses programmed circuit 4 (was 2); the sealed dome's Moon and Mars modes use circuits 5 and 6; the lunar quantum seed and the LiCl salt need circuit 1. Fixes recipes that sometimes vanished from the machines' lookup. Re-set the circuit in running greenhouses.

## What changes in your game

- New shapes only: no older multiblock changes shape. The Mk5 fusion reactor, the Penrose engine and the Neutronium Compression Hall are new structures.
- New dimensions with final ids: `grindless:rx_j1856_orbit`, `grindless:psr_j0437_orbit`, `grindless:cygnus_x1_orbit`. A relay platform is placed once per world by the first jump and is never moved or rebuilt by a later version.
- Nothing is removed. Existing saves keep every item, block and fluid. `highTierContent` stays off in `gtceu.yaml` (`highTierContent: false`, line 11): UIV hulls and hatches are registered by KubeJS. If it were turned on later, GTM would register the same `gtceu:uiv_*` ids and collide with ours.
- GT--'s optical mainframe recipe and GTM's metallic hydrogen cable and wire recipes are replaced.
- Tested on the bench: a world built with v0.13.0 opens with v0.14.0 and keeps its marked blocks, chest contents and the UEV energy tower (formed again, back on the wireless network) and still lists all ten station slots. In a cold restart of a v0.14.0 world the same marks survive, and the three arrival platform dimensions load and keep a force-loaded chunk and a chest. A placed relay platform itself was not part of that save run (no jump was run in it).
- Measured on the bench: a UIV base drew 565,127,276 EU/t or more for 60 s (639,106,096 EU/t fed by calibrated sources, the plants not built), put 607,150,791 EU/t into the bank and sent 536,870,912 EU/t out through a UIV 16 A hatch into a power substation.
- Some UIV items are shown in the recipe viewer for the first time; items from later eras stay hidden.
