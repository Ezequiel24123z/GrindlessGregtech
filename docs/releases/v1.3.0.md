Builds the owner's decisions on the audit (#230): a cheaper Hyperstargate, a denser MAX+ power plant, and wireless energy and the Hyperstargate per team.

- The Hyperstargate takes about 15,800 horizon compressed plates instead of 6.4 million: the plate is now a die that is not used up in seven press, assembler and loom recipes. (#233)
- New MAX+ generator, the polarized fusion stellarator: it burns a new spin-polarized fuel line and runs up to 64 recipes at once, 2^40 EU/t through eight 64 A wireless dynamo hatches, so one plant feeds a 2^39 EU/t machine. (#234, #239)
- Wireless energy works per FTB team: each team has its own network, store and tier, and a wireless cover cannot be placed on another team's claimed chunk. (#235)
- One Hyperstargate dial takes the whole team of the controller's owner across, and the dial, the horizon capacitor bank and relay platforms use that team's network. (#238)
- Three UIV quests follow the recipe order again. (#236)
- Every recipe script runs inside its error guard, so a failing script is logged and the others still load. (#240)

Upgrading: in a world from v1.2.0 the wireless network starts again with an empty store, and the Hyperstargate forgets who has crossed. A new world avoids both.
