Vanilla crafting is back, the Crafting Station works with EMI and the grid, and the play server keeps force-loaded chunks running offline.

- Vanilla crafting: GTM no longer removes the vanilla recipes for blocks (storage blocks, iron and glass), paper, TNT, flint and steel and charcoal, and manual compression works. Machine recipes stay as alternatives. Sand smelts into glass in a furnace again.
- Crafting Station (J/EMI Edition, our fork, patch gg2): EMI's recipe fill reads the station's grid, sides and hidden slots, the server fills it safely, and the result is looked up once per click instead of once per slot (less lag on the output).
- Server: FTB Chunks' force-loaded chunks keep ticking with no player online (default config).
- Quests and docs: the Glass and Sulfur quests no longer say sand cannot smelt or that flint and steel needs steel. No new world needed.

Upgrading: no new world needed. Restart the game so the instance updates. Existing worlds keep their config files; on a server whose `config/gtceu.yaml` already exists, set the vanilla-recipe flags (`hardIronRecipes`, `hardGlassRecipes` and the others under `recipes:`) to `false` by hand.
