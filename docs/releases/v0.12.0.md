UEV takes you beyond Proxima to Alpha Centauri, Barnard's Star and Sirius B and opens the gate to UIV. Mega machines arrive too: large multiblocks with big parallels, one or more per era.

- Two deep fusion drive motors (DFD Mk1 for level 9 rockets, Mk2 for level 10) open space stations 9 and 10.
- New multiblocks at each star include the nebula collector, core drill, carbonyl refinery and white dwarf spectrograph.
- Superheavy elements (flerovium to moscovium) come from the heavy-ion cyclotron, recoil separator and superheavy chemistry lab.
- Fusion Mk4 takes 16 UEV hatches, and wireless energy reaches UEV.
- Mega machines: Wire Works (LV), Polymer Works (MV), Coil Chemical Plant (HV), Ore Refinery (EV), Smelting Complex (IV), Mega Chemical Complex (LuV), Assembly Hall (ZPM), Mega Factory (UV), Quantum Fabricator (UHV), Stellar Forge (UEV).
- The Hyper (UHV) and Ultimate (UEV) Parallel Control Hatches give 1,024 and 4,096 parallels.
- New UEV Age quest chapter, mega machine quests in the LV to UHV chapters and a Multiblocks tab.
