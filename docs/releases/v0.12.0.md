## What's new

**UEV: three stars.** New multiblocks of ours take you beyond Proxima, to Alpha Centauri, Barnard's Star and Sirius B, and open the gate to UIV. Two new deep fusion drive motors (the DFD Mk1 for level 9 rockets, the DFD Mk2 for level 10) reach Alpha Centauri AB orbit and Barnard b, then Sirius B orbit; space stations 9 and 10 open there, and the UHV warp gate gains relay pairs at Alpha Centauri and Sirius. A new quest chapter (UEV Age), a UEV processing-lines chart and an updated Power tab come with it.

- **Isotopes:** the Calutron (UHV voltage) separates calcium-48, potassium-40, lithium-6 and plutonium-244; the IV fuel fabrication plant gains MOX (circuit 8), the HFIR plutonium-242 targets (circuit 4).
- **Superheavy elements:** the heavy-ion cyclotron, recoil separator and superheavy chemistry lab make flerovium, livermorium, tennessine, oganesson and moscovium.
- **Megabar press:** (La,Y)H10 superhydride (labelled fiction in EMI: the real one needs over 100 GPa) and nanotwinned diamond. (La,Y)H10 wire now comes only from UV's powder-in-tube line.
- **Single crystals:** the electron beam furnace refines 6N hafnium, tantalum, niobium and rhenium; the single-crystal foundry casts SX superalloy blades from Barnard's rhenium.
- **Composites:** the nitride magnet works (Fe16N2 magnets), the SiC fibre plant and the CVI furnace (SiC/SiC plates, W-fibre/W tiles). The HfTaCN coil block, the UEV hull and the UEV energy hatches become craftable.
- **Photonics:** the SNSPD foundry, the van der Waals stacker and the photonic integration hall; GT--'s optical computer now comes only from the hall.
- **Fusion Mk4:** the Mk4 fusion reactor (GTM fusion tier 10, 16 UEV hatches, 2.56 G EU buffer) and the helium-3 polariser (D-He-3, p-B11 and polarised D-He-3); the direct energy converter gains three Mk4 rows.
- **Earth orbit:** the quantum gas lab and the entanglement station (degenerate EOS model, quantum repeater node, lobster-eye optics, hollow-core fibre, TGG Faraday isolators, PPKTP, ULE cavities).
- **Wireless energy reaches UEV:** UEV hatches (2/4/16/64 A), covers and the UEV SMES cell (251.66e9 EU, made by the magnet works from four UHV cells).
- **Alpha Centauri:** in Alpha Centauri AB orbit the nebula collector scoops interstellar gas (a cryogenic split gives hydrogen-deuterium, helium and neon fractions) and catches interstellar dust in silica aerogel tiles; the interstellar dust lab extracts and demineralises the grains (a Fe-60 record among them), and the pulsar timing station turns pulsar timing data into the pulsar navigation chart the UEV gate needs. Two new Dyson swarm rows run at Alpha Centauri: a full Alpha swarm (12,582,912 EU/t a station) and a coronal probe-bay run.
- **Barnard b:** the Barnard core drill mines core rock (a gravity survey from Earth orbit quadruples the yield; a pilot run starts you off) and the carbonyl refinery runs the Mond process to iron pentacarbonyl, the carbonyl iron behind Fe16N2 magnets and the UEV motor. Barnard residue is the only rhenium source: the platinum plant leaches it to ammonium perrhenate on Barnard b, then hydrogen reduces it to rhenium, which opens the SX blade chain and the Brayton turbine wheel. The DFD Mk2 takes Barnard's magnets, its UEV motor and the Brayton wheel; the DFD Mk1 is built at home (its RF antenna is a UEV emitter).
- Sirius B orbit gets three multiblocks: the white dwarf spectrograph (mass-radius chart), the degenerate harvester and the containment works (degenerate pellet). The UEV field generator now has its recipe, which opens the Alpha Centauri nebula collector and the pulsar navigation chart.
- The gate to UIV: the UHV warp gate gains a hold load (circuit 3, 16 A UEV for 60 s, only in Sirius B orbit; the tennessine sample, degenerate pellet, EOS model and four optical computers are kept, not spent), and the UIV machine casing gets its assembly-line recipe, researched with the pulsar navigation chart.

**Mega machines.** Huge multiblocks with large parallels, one or more per era, each built from the machines it replaces. Every parallel copy pays its full EU, so the hatches you feed limit them.

- **Wire Works (LV):** wiremill and bender, up to 8 recipes.
- **Polymer Works (MV):** polyethylene and rubber plates from their raw inputs in one recipe, up to 16 parallels.
- **Coil Chemical Plant (HV):** large chemical reactor recipes with coil-scaled parallels up to 64, a coil EU discount and perfect overclock.
- **Ore Refinery (EV):** raw ore to dust in one recipe (macerate, wash with plain water, thermal centrifuge, macerate), every stage's energy summed, up to 64 parallels.
- **Smelting Complex (IV):** the electric blast furnace and vacuum freezer in one recipe per hot ingot, every stage's energy summed; parallels grow with the coil (up to 64) and GTM's blast furnace overclock and coil temperature rule still apply.
- **Mega Chemical Complex (LuV, 15x15x15):** the large chemical reactor plus whole PTFE, epoxy and para-aramid lines as one recipe each; parallels from the coil times the parallel hatch.
- **Assembly Hall (ZPM):** assembler and circuit assembler recipes with parallels; it stands inside a cleanroom and needs molten tin for circuit assembler recipes.
- **Mega Factory (UV, 15x12x21):** 15 machine types chosen by five module blocks (separation, forming, cutting, mixing, heat) placed in four sockets; parallels 256 times the parallel hatch's (65,536 with a UV hatch); every module installed costs 250 mB of PCB coolant per run.
- **Quantum Fabricator (UHV, 11x10x12):** assembler, circuit assembler, forming press, laser engraver and cutter, one mode at a time; 1,024 times the parallel hatch (1,048,576 with a UHV hatch), perfect overclock, no energy discount. It fits inside the largest cleanroom, which circuit recipes still need. New block: the field ring casing.
- **Hyper Parallel Control Hatch (UHV):** 1024 parallels, for every machine that takes a parallel hatch.
- **Ultimate Parallel Control Hatch (UEV):** 4,096 parallels, for every machine that takes a parallel hatch.
- **Stellar Forge (UEV):** a new 7 x 16 x 7 multiblock (heatproof casing, four coil rings) that runs blast furnace recipes; every 450 K its coils run above a recipe doubles the copies, up to the parallel hatch and the energy hatches. An optional 250 mB charge of UEV D-He-3 plasma makes a run 900 K hotter and returns helium-4 ash; a recipe hotter than the coils is never run.

Each era chapter from LV to UHV gains one quest for its mega machine, with a small reward.

**Multiblocks quest tab.** A new chapter in Quality of Life lists every craftable multiblock, ours first, in one band per voltage tier (Steam to UEV), as a visual catalogue. It has no dependencies and gives a small XP reward per controller.

## What changes in your game

- (La,Y)H10 wire: GTM's wiremill, extruder and shaped wire recipes are removed; it comes only from UV's powder-in-tube line.
- GT--'s optical computer recipe is removed; it comes only from the photonic integration hall.
- New shapes only: no older multiblock changes shape. The Mk4 fusion reactor is a new structure.
- New dimensions with final ids: `grindless:alpha_centauri_orbit`, `grindless:barnard_b`, `grindless:barnard_b_orbit`, `grindless:sirius_b_orbit`.
- Nothing is removed. Existing saves keep every item, block and fluid.
- Tested on the bench: a world created with v0.11.0 opens with v0.12.0 and still lists all ten station slots; in a cold restart of a v0.12.0 world the marked blocks, chest contents and the UHV energy tower (formed again, back on the wireless network) survive.
- The smelting complex's recipe ids lose a doubled path (grindless:smelting_complex/smelting_complex/... is now grindless:smelting_complex/...); the multiblock is new in this release, so no save is affected.
- Measured on the bench: a UEV base drew 141,281,819 EU/t or more for 60 s (169,344,048 EU/t fed by calibrated sources, the plants not built), put 160,876,846 EU/t into the bank and sent 134,217,728 EU/t out through a UEV hatch.
- Some UEV items are shown in the recipe viewer for the first time; items from later eras stay hidden.

## For contributors

- The player channel is now the `releases` branch (`playable` is mirrored during the transition; `jugable` is gone). See README and `.github/instructions/release.instructions.md`.
- Quest regeneration order: `node tools/quests/uev-chapter/gen.mjs` (the UEV chapter must exist first), then `tools\quests\processing-lines\run.ps1 -Dump <dump> -Write`, then `node tools/quests/multiblocks/gen.mjs --dump <dump> --write` (it restores the Multiblocks lint exceptions the processing-lines generator rewrites).
- `lib/mega.js` is the shared mega library (parallels, coil discount, overclock, batch, module sockets); see `docs/eras/mega.md`.
- The reachability validator covers UEV (`tools\analysis\Invoke-Reachability.ps1 -Tier uev`). It found that the DFD Mk1 needed Barnard to reach Barnard, and that Alpha Centauri's collector waited for Sirius B; both are fixed (the Mk1's antenna is a UEV emitter, the UEV motor goes into the Mk2; the collector takes UV field generators).
