Project Reforge from EV to MAX+: longer production lines on real processes, structures sized by use and era, a custom casing family for every era from UEV up, and charts and quests that teach them.

- EV to ZPM lines: tungsten through APT (#355); the E1 rare-earth line (#307); germanium from Io's fumaroles (#329); niobium (#330); nickel and the coated-conductor tape through the Mond process and an eight-stage tape line (#356); PEDOT:PSS and the microelectrode array for the organoid lab (#352, #371); ZBLAN from kiln fluorides (#366); PAN fibre (#364); A206 (#365); naquadah power (#362); the matter loop (#363); fuel (#318).
- UV and UHV lines: the thorium cycle from THOREX to U-233 (#317, #320); SRF cavities (#357); ultrapure water in five grades (#369); xenobiology from Proxima b (#368); the gate ring (#358); the ice works (#359); the Starshot (#312); light and small parts (#319, #339).
- UEV to MAX+ lines: photonics (#316), the optical mainframe (#323), spintronics (#331), SMES (#333), the cross-era links (#334), and the qubit stages from OpV to MAX+ (#336, #354, #381).
- Structures grow with use and era: EV to UHV halls at their target sizes (#367, #348, #340, #341, #347, #345, #346, #377, #378, #379), and every UEV to MAX+ structure reshaped in its era's casings (#322, #383, #325, #342, #328, #384, #327, #335, #337, #343, #338, #344, #370, #324). Merged machines: the Superheavy Factory (recoil separator), the Degenerate Works (containment works), the Spintronic Cluster Tool (oxide PLD chamber, racetrack fab), the Pasta Mill (draw bench, laminator), the vdW stacker (vdW stacking cell), the horizon crystal furnace (multi plasma blender).
- Casing families from UEV to MAX+, each made from the era before it, priced in their class bands (#310, #314, #315, #308, #321, #332, #361, #398). Costs re-solved on an honest base (#349).
- Charts: every era's processing-lines chart redrawn as one trunk per line, with the Si-28 qubit line followed across UXV, OpV, MAX and MAX+ (#372, #350, #373, #374, #376, #389, #390, #391, #351, #380, #395).
- Quests: the EV to MAX+ chapters teach the new lines, wafers, casings and multiblocks; structure quests give the shape and the Terminal passes (#385, #375, #386, #387, #388, #392, #393, #394, #382, #396, #397, #400, #405). Rate cards on the HV, LuV and MAX+ charts compare routes with figures checked against the game (#305).
- Audits: the ending (#326), overflow (#360), recipes (#353), tools (#313) and factory-load baselines (#306): the measured factories stay far inside the 20 TPS budget.
- Bootstrap: no machine needs its own products to be built (the Superheavy Factory, the Spintronic Cluster Tool, the membrane line and others were fixed while integrating); the pasta containment casing now walls the Vela pasta machines.
- Assembly lines: an ingredient scaled past 64 is now split across buses in stacks of 64, as GregTech writes its own recipes, so an ME input bus can feed every line (the LuV motor's 128 fine ruridit wire could not start before); totals are unchanged.
- Tests: the native fixtures follow the new shapes and the wireless network's strongest-cell rule (#399, #401 to #404, #406).

Upgrading: start a new world. Structures, casings and recipes changed throughout, and several machines were merged into others.
