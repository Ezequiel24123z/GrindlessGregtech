## What's new

This release answers the owner's first round of feedback on v1.0.1.

**One chapter per era.** Each tier is a single chapter of the quest book, in one Progression group, instead of five to eight chapters under a tab. Each chapter can be large, but every quest in it is reachable from the era's single start through real dependencies: no loose quests. Sections inside a chapter (circuits, machines, older machines and power, and so on) have a heading quest. Teaching texts keep their full length.

**Places only where they matter.** A recipe needs a dimension only when it uses that place:
- its ground, air, ice or fields;
- its microgravity, heat, light or radiation;
- a gravity well or a phenomenon;
- air for combustion.

Launches, gates and relays also keep their places. Everything made from shipped inputs is now made anywhere. For example, the quantum seeds (Venusian, Lunar, Martian, Hermean and the outer planets') are made anywhere, while the Hyperstargate still stands at the black hole.

**Any circuit of the tier.** Recipes take `#gtceu:circuits/<tier>`, so universal circuits and any circuit of that tier work. Only a circuit's own chain and research recipes name a specific circuit. The warp gate's holds keep tier circuits too.

**The prospector works as in GregTech 1.12.2.** Arrival gives a normal, fully charged GregTech HV prospector:
- right-click opens GregTech's own map, with the ore and fluid overlay, the vein list and the Xaero ore layer;
- one charge gives about 1,000 scans;
- recharge it in an HV charger.

**Wireless energy runs at its strongest cell.** One MAX cell in any tower feeds hatches and covers of every tier. A formed horizon capacitor bank counts as MAX.

**Creative flight with the electric suits.** A charged NanoMuscle or QuarkTech chestplate lets you fly. It drains the chestplate only while flying: 175 EU/t for the NanoMuscle family, about 30 minutes per plain charge, and 2,500 EU/t for the QuarkTech family. Both suits have a quest now.

**Readable recipe pages.**
- Dimension requirements show the place's icon and name, instead of a barrier and a raw key.
- Fuels that need air say so.
- Long recipe notes wrap inside the box.

**Productive Bees is removed.** It was an optional second route; no material depended on it.

**The Hyperstargate can be built.** Two creative-core recipes consumed 16 Horizon Shards each, but shards cannot be made. The shard is now a catalyst there, never used up.

## What changes in your game

A v1.0.1 world keeps working, and your quest progress is kept (quest ids did not change). Productive Bees blocks and items disappear: Forge warns about the missing entries when the world loads.

## For contributors

- Quest tools: one chapter per era (`docs/quests.md` section 4b), a connectivity lint, and the role `era` (120 words).
- Cost tree: `tools/analysis/cost-tree.mjs` shows any item's full cost as an interactive page.
- The new rules are AGENTS.md principles 10 and 11.

## Known risks

- Productive Bees blocks and items disappear from a v1.0.1 world; nothing in progression used them.
- The Hyperstargate's cost is not yet tuned to its design target (about 10 times the GTNH Stargate): compressed plates, containment plates and Kerr condensate are far above it, processors, shielding and conduit below. A rebalance waits on the owner's decision.
- Research pages (scanner and research station) show the researched item as the output. The real output is a data stick that carries the research; this is GregTech Modern's display.
- 8 long era notes on recipe pages still end in "...". In a very small window (854 x 480) the tallest pages (Warp Gate, Black Hole Bomb) are cut at the bottom; they fit at normal window sizes.
- Suit flight was proven in short flights on the test client (30 ticks per suit, drained every tick it flies).
- Known stale test checks (contributors only): `wireless_energy|recipes`, `luv_a4|mw_recipes`, `regolith_cell|recipes` and `|crafting`, `venus|controllers` and `|engine_recipe` already failed on v1.0.1; they go to the next audit.
