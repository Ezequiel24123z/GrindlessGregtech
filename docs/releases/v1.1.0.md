Answers the first round of feedback on v1.0.1.

- Each era is one quest chapter, in one group, with every quest reachable from the era's start. (#179, #190 to #194)
- Recipes need a dimension only when they use that place, and anything made from shipped inputs works anywhere. (#185 to #189)
- Recipes take any circuit of the tier, universal circuits included. (#185 to #189)
- The Arrival prospector is a normal, fully charged GregTech HV prospector: GregTech's own map, about 1,000 scans per charge. (#177)
- Wireless energy runs at the strongest cell: one MAX cell in any tower feeds every hatch and cover. (#182)
- A charged NanoMuscle or QuarkTech chestplate gives creative flight, draining 175 or 2,500 EU/t only while flying. Both suits have quests. (#181)
- Recipe pages show each dimension's icon and name, say when a fuel needs air, and wrap long notes. (#178)
- Productive Bees is removed. (#180)
- The Hyperstargate can be built: the Horizon Shard is now a catalyst, not consumed.

Upgrading: v1.0.1 worlds and quest progress carry over; Productive Bees blocks and items disappear and Forge warns about the missing entries.
