## What's new

**Project Reforge.** v1.0.0 reworks the whole pack from v0.17.0 and adds a last era, MAX+, with the Hyperstargate and the ending. Start a new world.

- **Costs climb with every era.** Key recipes and final items now cost far more items and fluids as you go up, with the same recipe times and EU/t. The target is a core-component cost of 3 x 2.5^n from MV, measured on the cheapest chain down to base resources; every era from MV to MAX lands within 10% of it (`docs/reforge/02_cost_curve.md`). LV is only reviewed. `integration/cost_curve.js` applies the factors from `tables/cost_curve/<era>.json`. The 3x3 crafting-table recipes of the core components from MV up are gone (they cannot scale); the MV and HV machine hulls stay as the bootstrap. OpV and MAX core components now take the previous tier's component, so they no longer skip the earlier lines.
- **Process lines that mean something.** Every era was audited line by line. Chains are real production lines of at least four steps that cross older lines; trivial steps were made real, and shortcuts that skipped a line were closed (for example GTM's tetrataenite ore steps and its assembler optical pipe). Capacity is checked too: no recipe needs more than its machine can hold.
- **A rebuilt quest book.** Each era has six roles: what's new, materials processing, wafers and circuits, machines, integration and a capstone. Steam to HV have one chapter each, EV to LuV three, ZPM to OpV four, MAX five and MAX+ eight, grouped by era (Early Ages for Steam to HV). A Processing Lines chart per era tracks every line step by step. Quests teach each wafer, circuit generation, process and multiblock; item rewards come only from lines the era's gate already required. Quests never block recipes.
- **Art and sound.** The 50 missing textures are drawn, the 21 controllers that shared one face have their own, and each era has its own style (Steam to MAX+, UV to UXV included). Every machine loop is mastered to one level, and 36 missing sounds were generated.
- **MAX after Reforge.** The Galactic Centre grows from 10 to 14 multiblocks (the horizon interferometer, the ergoregion synchroniser, the horizon flux clamp and the segment reclamation works) and 57 lines. The mirror shell no longer ends the game: it stabilises Sgr A*'s horizon and makes `grindless:horizon_lock`, the key to MAX+.
- **MAX+.** The last era, in three stages:
  - *Out of the Galaxy:* the Hills slingshot throws a probe from Sgr A* (gate c16) to 30 Doradus; the Magellanic Stream follows (c17).
  - *The Local Group:* M31's nucleus (c18) and the Local Group filament (c19).
  - *The Hyperstargate:* built at Sgr A* from about 1,200 grid crafts.

  In all: four new places (`grindless:tarantula_orbit`, `grindless:magellanic_stream`, `grindless:m31_nucleus_orbit`, `grindless:local_group_filament`), 17 multiblocks (the Hyperstargate is the last; the Hyperfactory mega comes on top) and 58 lines, with cost x5 per stage. MAX+ runs at MAX voltage with huge amperage: a 1,024 A MAX+ hatch, a x4,194,304 parallel hatch (`gtceu:max_maxplus_parallel_hatch`) and an exact wireless store for the dial's 2^43 EU/t.
  - Machines: the Hills slingshot, the Tarantula stellar lift, the pair-instability crucible, the nickel-56 burner, the pristine gas works, the primordial fusion torus, the blueshift forge, the void crystal fab, the meta-material loom, the multi-plasma blender, the horizon crystal furnace, the condensate assembler, the condensate refinery, the bound-matter press, the hyper assembly array and `gtceu:horizon_capacitor_bank`, and the Hyperstargate.
  - Creative items: six `grindless:creative_*` items are crafted from core lines and enormous quantities of everything below them. They are ingredients of the gate only and cannot be placed.
  - The Hyperstargate (41x43x9) is about ten times GTNH's Stargate in crafts and parts, scaled by quantity and chain depth, never by recipe time. Its dial draws 2^43 EU/t for 1,200 ticks, consumes a genome archive and keeps the horizon lock; power limits how often it dials.
  - Crossing it takes you to `grindless:horizon_city`, a final dimension of ruined cities with no machines. The first arrival rolls the credits and gives the epilogue book; the world continues.
  - AE2's top tier (MEGA 256M cells, ExtendedAE and Expanded AE parts) is re-gated behind MAX+ parts; lower routes stay.
- **English only.** Spanish names, tooltips and files are gone; every name, tooltip, EMI page and quest is English.
- **Cleanup.** Dead content went: the GT-- component assembly line and circuit families, the Expanded Assembly Line and Matrix Data Relay, trivial steps, orphan textures and every leftover made for old saves.

## What changes in your game

- **A new world is required.** There is no save compatibility (DECISIONS.md:701): ids, dimensions, multiblock shapes and quests changed. Do not open a v0.17.0 or older world.
- The ending moved from MAX to MAX+. The mirror shell's last recipe makes `grindless:horizon_lock` and no longer rolls the credits; the credits come with the first arrival in `grindless:horizon_city`.
- Removed: `grindless:universe_seed`, `grindless:launched_belt_seed`, `grindless:rejected_timing_head`, `gtceu:kerr_strand`, 25 save-compatibility items (the `grindless:{uev,uiv,uxv}` components and `vein_sample_scheelite`), GT--'s component assembly line recipes, the Expanded Assembly Line, the Matrix Data Relay and the two LuV wireless data hatches (hidden), GTM's 14 tetrataenite ore steps and its assembler optical pipe.
- Renamed: `grindless:max_epilogue` is `grindless:epilogue`, hidden until the MAX+ arrival.
- Gate c15 returns nothing, and the hot cell's tritium store runs at VA[UEV].
- The Spanish translation is gone (EN-1).
- Costs rise from MV up: expect much larger inputs in key recipes, and plan automation for them.

## Known risks

- Cost: LuV (0.912) and UIV (0.907) sit near the lower edge of the 10% band. The OpV and UXV hulls are outliers. Some routes still multiply matter (ash separation fed by diamond CVD, blue topaz ore at ZPM and later).
- UHV's ZBLAN mixer row takes 73,250 mB of fluorine, more than a singleblock mixer holds; the large mixer runs it.
- EMI: 8 multiblock previews are listed as runtime gaps (likely an audit false positive that the next 8 GB EMI run should clear).
- Sound loop seams and harsh flags need an in-game listen; 220 multiblocks have no loop of their own.
- The `maxplus` stage is a world flag (`grindless_stage_maxplus`), since the pack has no stage mod.
- GCYR's configured low gravity is not applied in game.
- No player has played the pack yet; balance at the new costs is measured by tools, not by play.
