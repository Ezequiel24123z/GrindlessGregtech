Project Reforge reworks the whole pack from v0.17.0 and adds a last era, MAX+, with the Hyperstargate and the ending.

- Costs climb with every era from MV, aiming at 3 x 2.5^n for core components with the same recipe times and EU/t. The 3x3 crafting recipes of core components are gone, except the MV and HV hulls. (#46, #64, #78, #88, #105, #116)
- Process lines were audited: chains are real production lines of at least four steps, and shortcuts like GTM's tetrataenite ore steps are closed. (#47, #48, #77, #91)
- The quest book is rebuilt: six roles per era, a Processing Lines chart for each, and one chapter per era for Steam to HV up to eight for MAX+. (#97, #98, #136, #165)
- The 50 missing textures are drawn, 21 controllers got their own face, each era has its own style, and 36 missing sounds were generated. (#52 to #55, #96)
- MAX grows from 10 to 14 multiblocks and 57 lines, and the mirror shell now makes the horizon lock, the key to MAX+. (#65 to #68, #82, #99)
- MAX+ adds four places, 17 multiblocks and 58 lines, a 1,024 A hatch and a x4,194,304 parallel hatch. (#104, #123, #137, #138)
- Crossing the Hyperstargate (41x43x9, dial at 2^43 EU/t for 1,200 ticks) leads to Horizon City; the first arrival rolls the credits and gives the epilogue book. (#145, #148, #152, #155)
- AE2's top tier (MEGA 256M cells, ExtendedAE and Expanded AE parts) now needs MAX+ parts. (#120)
- The pack is English only, and dead content is gone: GT--'s component assembly line, the Expanded Assembly Line, the Matrix Data Relay and leftovers for old saves. (#57, #146, #159, #163)

Upgrading: a new world is required. Do not open a v0.17.0 or older world.
