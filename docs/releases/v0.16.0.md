## What's new

**OpV: the galactic bulge.** Eighteen new multiblocks of ours turn antimatter into power and tools at home and at Cygnus X-1, then follow a relay to two new places: the magnetar SGR 1806-20 and the Terzan 5 core. They end at the gate to MAX. A new quest chapter (52 quests) and a new OpV processing-line chart (304 quests) guide the route.

- Route: tetron circuits and census IV at home; antiprotons, strangelets and the Kugelblitz forge at Cygnus X-1 orbit; a bulge relay seed thrown by the warp gate (circuit 11) to SGR 1806-20, where chain matter and the field-frozen press make the OpV components; a second relay (circuit 12) to Terzan 5, where von Neumann replication and the bulge collector run.
- New multiblocks (size W x H x D): tetron fab 9x7x11, gamma optics works 7x7x15, antihydrogen foundry 9x9x13, strangelet vault 9x9x9, annihilation engine 11x11x11, jet beam line 5x5x21, spallation target station 9x7x13, antiproton decelerator 13x5x13, strangelet distiller 7x7x13, Kugelblitz forge 15x15x15, Hawking collector 11x11x11, magnetic pair converter 9x9x9, field-frozen press 7x9x7, debris harvester 11x5x11, replication refinery 9x9x9, von Neumann foundry 11x11x11, collector node works 9x5x13, bulge collector 13x7x13. Each forms from its own EMI preview.
- New places (final ids): `grindless:sgr1806_orbit` and `grindless:terzan5_core`. Warp gate circuits c11 (SGR 1806-20), c12 (Terzan 5) and c13 (the MAX throw, `hold_sgr_a`) are new; c1 to c10 are unchanged.
- Power: annihilation engine (up to 1,073,741,824 EU/t), Hawking collector and bulge collector (c3 runs 64 nodes). Every generator burns a stated fuel and returns 1.3x to 2.5x its cost; none exceeds 2^30 EU/t.
- Wireless case 13 (OpV SMES cell, 6,144 UV batteries) and the OpV parallel control hatch (x262,144). The gate to MAX needs 2^33 EU/t for 1,200 ticks, paid by the SMES precharge.
- OpV components (GTM's nine `gtceu:opv_*` cores), the 37 OpV basic machines (assembler rows), the cosmic mainframe and 45 process lines (216 steps, 141 crossings, 49 machines).
- Older machines gain new modes only (pasta machines at SGR, hot cell c22, refinery, platinum plant and others).

## What changes in your game

- The warp gate's item input slots go from 7 to 9. Its shape does not change, so a built gate keeps working; the new slots appear in its controller.
- New multiblock shapes: only the 18 machines above. No older multiblock changes shape.
- Nothing is removed. New dimension ids are final. The warp gate's ride-home orbits now include SGR 1806-20 and Terzan 5.
- Quests: a new OpV age chapter and OpV process lines; no existing quest changes.
