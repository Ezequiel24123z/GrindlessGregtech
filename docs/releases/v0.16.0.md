OpV adds eighteen multiblocks that turn antimatter into power and tools at home and at Cygnus X-1, then follow a relay to the magnetar SGR 1806-20 and the Terzan 5 core. They end at the gate to MAX.

- Route: tetron circuits at home; antiprotons, strangelets and the Kugelblitz forge at Cygnus X-1; chain matter and the field-frozen press at SGR 1806-20; von Neumann replication and the bulge collector at Terzan 5.
- Warp gate circuits 11 and 12 throw the relay seeds; circuit 13 is the throw to MAX, 2^33 EU/t for 1,200 ticks paid by the SMES precharge.
- Power: annihilation engine (up to 1,073,741,824 EU/t), Hawking collector and bulge collector. Generators return 1.3x to 2.5x their cost; none exceeds 2^30 EU/t.
- Wireless energy reaches OpV with an OpV SMES cell (6,144 UV batteries) and an OpV parallel control hatch (x262,144).
- New OpV quest chapter (52 quests) and an OpV processing-line chart (304 quests).
- The warp gate's item input slots go from 7 to 9; its shape does not change, so a built gate keeps working.
