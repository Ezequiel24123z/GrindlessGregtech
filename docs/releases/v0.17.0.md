## What's new

**MAX: the Galactic Centre.** Ten new multiblocks of ours carry the pack from the galactic bulge to Sagittarius A* and its ending. A gate at Sgr A* consumes the belt seed and opens a second new place, the NSC belt; the last machine, the mirror shell, is the final multiblock of the pack.

- Route: the Sgr A* probe (warp gate circuit 14), the Rydberg and interferometer works, the membrane line, the gene ark and the photon ring imager at home and at Sgr A*; the belt seed (circuit 15) to the NSC belt for the cluster-light furnace; the Geroch winch, the mass stream injector and the accretion feeder; the mirror shell last.
- New multiblocks: `gtceu:rydberg_array_works` 9x7x9, `gtceu:atom_interferometer_works`, `gtceu:membrane_line`, `gtceu:gene_ark`, `gtceu:photon_ring_imager` 17x9x17, `gtceu:geroch_winch` 9x33x9, `gtceu:mass_stream_injector` 15x7x15, `gtceu:mirror_shell` 33x33x33, `gtceu:cluster_light_furnace` 13x9x13, `gtceu:accretion_feeder` 15x9x15 (Sgr A* orbit).
- New places: `gcyr:black_hole_orbit` and `grindless:nsc_belt`. Warp gate circuits c14 (`hold_sgr_a_probe`, 2^33 EU/t) and c15 (`hold_nsc_belt`, 2^33 EU/t for 1,200 ticks on two OpV 16 A hatches) are new; c1 to c13 are unchanged.
- Power: the Geroch winch (2^30 EU/t on one MAX 1 A or OpV 4 A dynamo), the accretion feeder and the mirror shell's first recipe are generators; each burns a stated fuel and returns 1.97x to 2.3x its cost; none exceeds 2^30 EU/t.
- MAX parts: the tier 14 hatches (including the parallel control hatch, x1,048,576, and the 64 A wireless hatch), the MAX components, the horizon strand cable and 46 process lines (232 steps, 167 crossings, 71 older machines).
- MAX casings (stream turn, optical table, membrane roller, Geroch drum) are now craftable as assembler rows at VA[MAX].
- Quests: the chapter "MAX - The Galactic Centre" (53 quests, xp rewards only), plus MAX process lines.
- The ending: the mirror shell's last recipe (2^35 EU/t, 4.12e13 EU) rolls the credits once per player and gives the epilogue book (`grindless:max_epilogue`); the title, the book and the last quest are the fallback content.

- The aerogel works accepts up to 3 input items; the assembly hall runs on two OpV 4 A hatches.
- The Geroch winch has one circuit (the tether rework is an assembler row); the released AlGaAs film is a chemical reactor row (L33).

- New multiblock shapes: only the ten machines above. No older multiblock changes shape.
- Nothing is removed. New dimension ids are final. A gate at Sgr A* consumes the belt seed and the launched GC probe.
- The mirror shell scans a 33-cube on the server; keep it clear of other machines.
