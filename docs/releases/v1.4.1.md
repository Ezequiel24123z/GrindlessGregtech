Fixes from the second global audit of v1.4.0. Worlds from v1.4.0 carry over.

- GTM's one-step vinyl chloride, TFE from chloroform and N2O4 straight from ammonia are gone from the large chemical reactor too. PVC now always goes through EDC, PTFE through R22, and N2O4 through nitrogen dioxide. A removed chemical reactor recipe can no longer stay alive in the large reactor. (#279)
- Cerium no longer comes from bastnasite in an MV electrolyzer: that electrolysis now needs EV, like the other rare earths. (#283)
- Quests:
  - iron sinter is made in the Steam Alloy Smelter, and the primitive blast furnace keeps its slow steel;
  - Aerozine 50 asks for its bucket;
  - the belt's quantum seed waits for the belt's rock;
  - the Power tab describes every source and machine. (#280)
- GT--'s chemical plant: its tooltip and the anthraquinone quest give the whole HV build (aluminium frostproof plant casings, HV machine casings, steel pipe casings, cupronickel coils). You do not need the titanium pipes the preview shows. (#280)
- The HV Processing Lines chart draws PTFE from chloroform and methane. The design document now counts line lengths the same way as the line audit. (#281)
- EMI:
  - eighteen recipe descriptions that never appeared now show;
  - two MTJ sputtering texts name the right circuits;
  - unused hatch textures are gone. (#282, #286)
- Byproducts: the pulsar wind trap's electrons are a stated vent, and the samarium line no longer leaves calcium fluoride that nothing can store. (#283)
- The server guide gives realistic download and first-boot times. The releases-channel zip ships the bootstrap's full licence text.

Upgrading: worlds from v1.4.0 carry over. Large chemical reactors set up for one-step vinyl chloride, TFE or N2O4, and MV electrolyzers that split bastnasite, need the new route.
