## What's new

**Machines that sound like themselves.** 80 more multiblocks of ours now play their own working loop (generated with ElevenLabs, then mastered to the pack's level), instead of GregTech's generic arc, motor or turbine. 185 of our 310 multiblocks now have their own sound.

- MAX+: all 16 machines that run their own recipes. The Hyperstargate's dial has the most distinctive loop of the pack.
- MAX: the horizon interferometer, the ergoregion synchroniser, the horizon flux clamp and the segment reclamation works.
- UIV: every machine with its own recipe type (21).
- The signature machine of more lines: UEV 14, UHV 12, ZPM 5, OpV 5, UV 3.

## What changes in your game

Nothing but the sounds. A v1.0.0 world keeps working: there is no new world to start this time. Your instance updates itself on the next launch.

## For contributors

Sound generation follows `docs/quality/sounds.md` with `tools/assets/New-MachineSound.ps1` (package SND-1, #176). One loop per recipe type of ours; GTM's own types keep GTM's sounds.

## Known risks

- Machines that use GregTech's own recipe types (the Hyperfactory, the fusion reactors) or have no recipes (the horizon capacitor bank) keep GregTech's sounds.
- Most machines below UEV that are not the signature of their line still use generic sounds.
- Some loops have audit flags (bright high end, a level step at the seam) that need a listen in game.
