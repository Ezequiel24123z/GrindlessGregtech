UV takes you to the edge of the Solar System with thirty-one new multiblocks: Neptune, Pluto, the Kuiper Belt and the heliopause.

- A level 7 rocket (elite motors, new BNNT fuel tank) reaches Neptune (tier 6) and Pluto (tier 7), each with its own orbit and station slot.
- At home: thorium and uranium-233, synchrotron, muon fusion cell, antimatter trap, fibre draw tower, ark biosphere, thruster works.
- Neptune orbit: deep siphon, Triton geyser sampler. Pluto orbit: Kuiper interceptor bay, amorphous ice annealer, penetrator probe bay.
- The precursor probe bay launches a probe past the heliopause, and the event horizon interferometer images Sagittarius A*.
- Starshot: light sail fab, starchip fab and a laser sail launcher on the Moon send a probe to Proxima.
- Fusion Reactor Mk3 makes neutronium (americium + naquadah), found nowhere else. The laser fusion chamber burns decaborane-based fuels.
- Wireless energy reaches UV (hatches, covers, UV SMES cell), and the quest book gets a UV chapter and a Power tab for UV.
