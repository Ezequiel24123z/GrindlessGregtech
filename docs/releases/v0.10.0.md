## What's new

**UV: Neptune, Pluto and the edge of the Solar System.** Thirty-one new multiblocks of ours take you to Neptune and Triton, Pluto and Charon, the Kuiper Belt and the heliopause, with the Fusion Reactor Mk3, a laser-sail probe to Proxima, and the UV tier of the wireless network. A new quest chapter and an updated Power tab come with it.

- **Getting there:** a level 7 rocket (elite motors and the new BNNT fuel tank, wound on the cryotank winder). Neptune (tier 6) and Pluto (tier 7) each get their own orbit and station slot.
- **At home:** thorium and uranium-233 (pyroprocessing cell, accelerator-driven reactor), heat sources, cryogenics, accelerators (synchrotron, muon fusion cell, antimatter trap), photonics (fibre draw tower, MOCVD), detectors (zone refining tower, optical ground station), new materials (BNNT yarn, printed parts), life (ark biosphere, neural culture vat), propulsion (cryotank winder, thruster works) and the starshot (light sail fab, starchip fab, laser sail launcher on the Moon).
- **Neptune and Pluto:** the Neptune deep siphon (deep hydrogen and ices; deuterium and helium-3 from the Saturn isotope column) and the Triton geyser sampler (nitrogen, Triton ice core) in Neptune orbit; the Kuiper interceptor bay and amorphous ice annealer in Pluto orbit (Kuiper noble gas, split into argon, krypton and xenon on the Moon); the penetrator probe bay (Sputnik Planitia and Charon cores) and volatile ice fractionator (nitrogen, carbon monoxide, methane, tholin) in Pluto orbit, where the HV Syngas Plant's new circuit 5 shifts Pluto's carbon monoxide into hydrogen.
- **The edge:** from Pluto orbit, the precursor probe bay launches a probe past the heliopause (heliopause data, which also aims the Starshot laser sail), and the event horizon interferometer images Sagittarius A* (the Sgr A* image).
- **Fusion:** the Fusion Reactor **Mk3** (GTM's own recipes, audited): plutonium-241 gives americium plasma for the plasma turbine, and americium + naquadah gives **neutronium**, made only here. Beside it, the **laser fusion chamber** burns decaborane-based fuels.
- **Wireless energy reaches UV:** UV hatches, covers and the UV SMES cell, made by the Naquadria Magnet Works. The network runs at UV when every formed tower holds UV cells. Tested on the bench: an 8,388,608 EU/t UV base ran for 60 s on a network fed 9,960,496 EU/t, with 9,462,471 EU/t reaching the bank.
- **Quest book:** a new chapter, **UV - Pluto and the Edge**, and the **Power** tab now covers UV. The UV Lines chapter shows the era's processing lines.

## What changes in your game

- The UHV energy hatch is now researched in the research station with the **Sgr A* image** (128 CWU/t); its assembly line inputs are unchanged.
- GTCA's space elevator and its modules stay hidden until UHV (GTCA gives them no recipe; UHV builds it).
- Nothing is removed. No older multiblock changes shape, and existing saves keep every item, block and fluid.
- Tested on the bench: a world created with v0.9.0 opens with v0.10.0 and keeps its blocks, chest contents, ZPM energy tower (formed again, back on the wireless network) and saved data; all six station orbits load, Neptune's and Pluto's included.
- Some UV items are shown in the recipe viewer for the first time; items from later eras stay hidden.
