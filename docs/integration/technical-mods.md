# Technical and magical mods: integration with GregTech

Draft for the orchestrator. Design only: nothing has been installed and no script has been written. Based on the user's vision (everything good gets in, but through GregTech) and on what Monifactory, TerraFirmaGreg, Star Technology, CTNH and Cosmic Frontiers do. Versions were checked on Modrinth on 2026-10-24 (1.20.1, Forge); everything else is *(to verify)*.

## Integration rules

1. **What adds a language GT does not have gets in** (mechanics and trains, programmable logic, magic). Anything that repeats a GT machine gets out; if two do the same thing, the better one stays.
2. **The gate is made of GT parts.** The 3–6 items that unlock a mod are remade with a circuit (`#gtceu:circuits/<tier>`), hull, motor and tier plates: they are removed by ID or type and re-added in the GT assembler, with the same item ID (Star Tech, Monifactory). The tables live in `data/mod_gates.js` and the recipes are generated by a loop.
3. **Energy in EU only.** With `nativeEUToFE` (4 FE per EU), GT cables already power FE consumers and the FE→EU converters are active (`config/gtceu.yaml`). That is why no mod can add a generator: it would be free EU (like the honey generator from Productive Bees, already removed). Create, mana, Source and pressure have their own energy and are not converted.
4. **Ore and processing belong to GT.** Foreign recipes that crush, wash, inject or dissolve ore are removed, along with ore generators (Orechid, Create veins, mining spirits) and cheap alloys. Only our own drills produce ore automatically.
5. **One material only.** Almost Unified with `gtceu` first and tags: no second copper, no zinc, no brass.
6. **Mobs go to the simulator.** Spawners, mob crushers and pits from other mods are out. The XP comes from the simulator, not from killing.
7. **Parallel lane, never a mandatory gate.** No recipe on the main route goes through these mods (mv.md already says so for Create).
8. **One `integration/<mod>.js` per mod**, with a header that says what is removed and why (like `pipez.js` and `productivebees.js`). What is removed is also hidden in EMI (client script, like Monifactory's nukelists).

## How they get in

### Create + Greate (LV to EV)

- **Provides:** contraptions, belts, arms and deployers, trains, mechanical crafting and sequenced assembly. It is the visual automation language GT does not have. Greate puts GT tiers on shafts, cogwheels, wheels, presses, mixers and saws. Create bundles Flywheel, Ponder and Registrate inside (Modrinth requires no dependencies).
- **Gate:** Create opens at LV (steam already has its multiblocks): the precision mechanism, deployer, arm and crafter require an LV circuit and an LV motor or arm made in the assembler. Andesite via the GT mixer, belts with rubber from the greenhouse (not algae), casings with a brass or steel plate. Greate tiers by shaft material: andesite → steel (LV) → aluminium (MV) → stainless steel (HV) → titanium (EV); they are capped there, as TFG does (`AVAILABLE_GREATE_MATERIAL_TIERS`, capacity 32, 128, 512, 2 048 and 8 192 SU). Greate's crafting recipes are removed and remade in the assembler (TFG); first try `enableHardCreateRecipes = true`.
- **Removed or rerouted:**
  - Create worldgen: `zinc_ore`, `striated_ores_overworld` and `striated_ores_nether` (biome_modifier → `forge:none` in the KubeJS datapack). GTM only removes vanilla ore.
  - Create's 1.20.1 branch includes 82 `create:crushing` recipes (55 for ore or `raw_*`), 47 `milling` and 17 `splashing`. The `raw_*` ones use the `forge:raw_materials/<metal>` tag and only require that the tag exists, so with GT almost all of them stay active (iron, copper, gold, zinc, tin, lead, silver, nickel…): the crushing wheel crushes GT's raw ore, gives free XP and skips the chain. Washing `crushed_raw_iron` gives 9 nuggets and 0.75 redstone, which GT does not give. All `create:crushing` is removed (Monifactory too), along with the `splashing` of `crushed_raw_*` and its smelting into ingots; `milling` is only kept for plants and bone *(to review)*.
  - Greate: `ignoredRecipeTypes = [gtceu:macerator, gtceu:ore_washer]`. No Create or Greate machine processes ore or grinds GT materials; forming (bender → press), cutting (cutter → saw), mixing and compacting do. `disableConflictingRecipes = true` and `useCreateItemsInRecipes = false` (config `greate-recipes`).
  - Hand crank (abused in contraptions, as Monifactory notes) and brass or zinc mixes that give a second ingot (AU + tags, not a global `replaceInput`).
- **Power:** SU is not converted: no alternator or converter, so CCA is out. Create's wheels, windmills and steam engine only drive Create and Greate machines; Greate limits 8 wheels per network and the RPM by shaft material. GT steam and Create steam (Steam 'n' Rails boilers) are separate networks. Optional: Create Liquid Fuel so that blaze burners use GT fuels (`blaze_burner_fuel` data from CTNH).
- **Risks:** Greate (0.0.x, in development) converts GT recipes on load with a mixin into `RecipeManager.apply`; those recipes cannot be removed by input, only by ID or output. Other mods that touch recipe loading (FastSuite, ModernFix) *(to verify)*. It slows reloads and fills EMI with recipes. Material ID collision with GTCA and GT-- *(to verify at startup)*. Flywheel with Oculus and shaders *(test on client)*. **Plan B** if the test bench rejects it: Create without processing, as Monifactory does (remove `create:crushing`, `milling` and `pressing`; keep crafter, deployer, belts and trains).

### Create add-ons

- **Steam 'n' Rails (LV–HV):** steel and rubber tracks in LV, bogies with an MV motor, locomotives with an HV circuit and stainless steel. Its boilers come from Create.
- **Stock Bridge (LV):** LV circuit plus AE2 parts. **Hypertube (MV):** brass and MV circuit (Star Tech: assembler at 30 EU/t).
- **Enchantment Industry (MV):** blaze enchanter and disenchanter with an MV circuit and steel. The XP comes from a mob simulator recipe into XP fluid (idea from Monifactory) *(to verify fluid ID)*. The XP from Create's experience nuggets and any recipe that forces killing are removed.

### Integrated Dynamics (MV)

- **Provides:** variables, readers, writers and operators. It reads what is inside GT machines through capabilities, without redstone cables. Requires Cyclops Core (already in via Colossal Chests) and Common Capabilities.
- **Gate:** logic programmer, readers and proxy with an MV circuit; Tunnels with HV.
- **Removed:** squeezer and drying basin (ore with extras; Monifactory removes them by ID), menril goes through GT's extractor, solidifier and mixer, and the coal generator and battery (FE).

### Plants: Botany Pots, Productive Bees and Trees

- **Botany Pots (LV):** pots made with GT steel. Only flowers, mushrooms, berries, dyes and food; trees, wood and resin still belong to the greenhouse, which gains yield from circuits and fertilizer. Soils with GT fertilizer. Data for `botanypots:crop` and `soil` from KubeJS.
- **Productive Bees and Trees (MV):** decided. Trees is beta and All Rights Reserved: breeding and 160 decorative woods; if it brings resource trees, they are removed *(to verify)*.

### Botania (MV to HV)

- **Provides:** mana, spreaders, generating flowers and automatable gardens; it is magic with visible "cables". Curios and Patchouli are already in.
- **Gate:** manasteel is infused with a GT steel ingot, not an iron one; spreader and pool require an MV circuit; the terra plate requires a pearl from the simulator, a GT diamond and an HV circuit. Elven items and Gaia: EV or higher.
- **Removed:** `botania:orechid` (16 recipes) and `orechid_ignem` (3): ore from stone and mana (CTNH rewrites them with GT ores; here it does not). Of the 139 in `mana_infusion`, the conjuration `*_dupe` recipes are removed (coal, redstone, glowstone, quartz, gravel, netherrack, snow, soul sand) and so are those that change materials GT controls (`flint_to_gunpowder`, `glowstone_dust_to_redstone`, `redstone_to_glowstone_dust`, `ender_pearl_from_ghast_tear`, `rotten_flesh_to_leather`). Also the Mana Fluxfield (FE). IDs are from the 1.20.x branch.
- **Power:** own mana. If mana is ever wanted as an input to a GT recipe, CTNH does it with a Java mod; here it does not.

### Occultism (MV to HV)

- **Provides:** spirits, rituals and familiars. Geckolib and Curios are already in; Modonomicon and Smartbrainlib are still missing.
- **Gate:** rituals with MV GT gems and plates (certus, silver, aluminium), as Cosmic Frontiers does with `gtceu:certus_quartz_gem`.
- **Removed:** `occultism:crushing` (181 recipes that give 2 dusts per raw ore, active for every GT metal whose tag exists), `occultism:miner` (86 ore tables) and the miner and dimensional mine rituals (Cosmic Frontiers keeps them; here they are magical ore). Dimensional storage is hidden: AE2 covers it.

### Optional: Ars Nouveau, PneumaticCraft, Flux Networks, Re:Avaritia

- **Ars Nouveau (HV):** it is remade with GT parts, as in Cosmic Frontiers (enchanting apparatus, pedestals and arcane core with a stainless steel plate and an HV circuit). Drygmy (mob farm), the amethyst golem and any ritual or familiar that gives ores or drops are removed *(to verify IDs)*. Its 26 `crush` recipes are dyes and stone, so they do not need to be touched.
- **PneumaticCraft (HV):** the oil lakes, the refinery and the plastic mixer are removed (GT does petrochemistry and polymers), the pneumatic dynamo (free FE) too, and compressed iron is remade with GT steel. Pressure chamber with stainless steel and an HV circuit.
- **Flux Networks (LuV):** dust via the GT Chemical Bath and core in an LuV assembler (Monifactory). Only with confirmed distribution.
- **Re:Avaritia (UEV+):** without Neutron Collector or Compressor (GT has neutronium); the infinity catalyst comes from an assembly line with research.

### GCYR and Almost Unified

- **GCYR:** the road to space (LGPL-3.0, adaptable with attribution). Its rockets require kapton and stainless steel at 480 EU/t (seen in batch 6): they open in EV, not HV (hv.md). Ad Astra is out: its generators and oxygen duplicate GT and its planets are in GCYR.
- **Almost Unified:** it was ruled out when only GTM was present; with Create and Greate it is needed. `config/almostunified/unify.json` with `gtceu` first *(to verify keys)*. It avoids Cosmic Frontiers' global `replaceInput`.

## Energy and bridges

- **EU ↔ FE:** `nativeEUToFE` and `euToFeRatio = 4` power FE consumers from GT cables; the converters use `feToEuRatio = 4`. As long as there are no foreign generators, that bridge gives nothing away.
- **Generators removed from the mods that get in:** Botania (Mana Fluxfield), Integrated Dynamics (coal generator and battery), PneumaticCraft (pneumatic dynamo) and Productive Bees (honey generator, already removed). In the mods that do not get in, the generator is part of the reason (Thermal, Mekanism Generators, Immersive Engineering, CCA, New Age).
- **Create SU:** its own network, with no bridge to EU or steam. CTNH does have a kinetic bridge in Java (CT++); here SU and EU are not mixed.
- **Mana, Source and pressure:** their own. Magic can be a raw material for GT chains (Cosmic Frontiers) but it never makes GT ores, ingots, circuits or fluids.

## Risks and open questions

- **Load order and recipes:** Greate and Almost Unified rewrite recipes on load. The order must be pinned against FastSuite and ModernFix, and reload time measured before and after batch A.
- **Worldgen:** after each batch, `gg_test_worldgen` is repeated (batch 6 baseline: 96 471 GT ore blocks, 0 vanilla). Watch Create, Occultism (iesnium ore) and Botania.
- **EMI:** Greate and what is hidden add recipes and leave orphans behind; they are checked with the tier's static validation (`Invoke-Reachability.ps1`).
- **Versions:** Create 6.0.8 and Botania 456 are the latest on Modrinth for 1.20.1. Thermal (2024) and Mekanism (2025-05) are frozen; Ars Nouveau 4.12.7 (2025-06) and Productive Trees 0.2.6 (2024-08) are not being updated.
- **Distribution:** Flux Networks is not on Modrinth. `packwiz cf add` only if its page allows redistribution (AGENTS.md).
- **Open questions on IDs:** the Ars Nouveau and Occultism IDs (rituals, familiars) have not been checked against the jars; they are read on the test bench before each `integration/<mod>.js` is written.

## Proposed decisions

1. **Create and Greate get in at LV** and the kinetic lane goes up to EV (andesite, steel, aluminium, stainless steel, titanium). They do not process ore: Greate ignores `gtceu:macerator` and `gtceu:ore_washer`, and Create's ore recipes are removed.

## Change of 2026-10-24 (afternoon): batches A–D are out

The pack becomes a journey to the black hole (`docs/journey.md`), and the user asks that each mod make sense in it and be interesting.

**Create and Greate are out.**
- They do not process ore.
- Their energy is not converted to EU.
- AE2 already does logistics and autocrafting from LV.
- Greate duplicates GT machines with other energy, and it copied GT recipes before our scripts did.

Batch A was tested and merged into `main`, but it is reverted before the next release and never reached the playable channel.

**Batches B, C and D do not get in either:**
- trains;
- Integrated Dynamics, Botany Pots and Productive Trees;
- Botania and Occultism.

They repeat what GT, AE2 and our greenhouses already do, or they do not fit the journey. What this document removes still applies.
