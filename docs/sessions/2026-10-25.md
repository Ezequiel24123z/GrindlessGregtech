# Session 2026-10-25

## What works

- **v0.5.0 "The Moon"** and **v0.5.1 "Quest book"** are released on GitHub and on the `jugable` channel.
  - The Moon at the end of HV: stainless rocket and suit, lunar drill, titanium and helium-3 only from the Moon, and the Earth–Moon AE2 bridge.
  - The whole quest book was rewritten: 319 quests in 11 chapters. Six cheap writers ran in parallel, then one stronger reviewer checked the logic of the LV, MV and HV chapters. Every chapter loads in game with its exact quest and dependency counts.
- **v0.5.2 content, tested on the bench (`release-0.5.2-pre1`, `-pre2`):**
  - **Power, closer to GregTech Leisure:**
    - Small boilers make x8 steam and large boilers have an x8 heat ceiling (`pack/config/gtceu.yaml`).
    - Every generator recipe runs twice as long on the same fuel (`power/generators.js`). The recipes keep their ids and their fluid tags.
  - **Cable-free power at LV:** the ExtendedAE wireless connector now needs an LV circuit, and EU P2P tunnels carry GT power over the ME network.
  - **Six cross-links between the LV–HV lines** (complexity audit). GTM's recipe lookup reaches every recipe of the touched types.
  - **Steam families** (ideas from Leisure, our own code and textures):
    - six small steam multiblocks with 8 parallels;
    - five Large Steam multiblocks with 64 parallels.
    - All eleven form on their own. The small compressor and chemical bath process 8 recipes at once.
  - **Validator:** steam, LV, MV and HV pass on the new dump, including the era rule and the obtainability audit.
- **The repository is in English.** Docs, comments, tool messages and commit messages are English, and the docs folders have English names. The `es_es` game translation stays (user decision).
- **The growth and power rules are pack rules now** (rules 7 and 8 of `AGENTS.md`, so every session sees them first).
  - Rule 7: each era has more of our progression multiblocks (about ×1.35 per era from EV on), more lines and more crossings. Parallel convenience multiblocks are counted apart.
  - Rule 8: power is generous but never free.
  - EV was raised from 7 to 11 multiblocks of ours in `docs/eras/ev.md`: combined-cycle plant, hot strip mill, sealed dome and rare-earth extraction cascade. The audit in `docs/research/complexity.md` follows the new target.
