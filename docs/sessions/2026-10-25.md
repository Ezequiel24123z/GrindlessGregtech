The ones that lose a route or run the wrong recipe (anthracene, the platinum sludge typo, bomb and dynamite, trinium carbonite) are being fixed; the harmless duplicates are accepted. Next, the validator will drop recipes the lookup can't reach.
- **Growth and power are pack rules** (rules 7 and 8 of `AGENTS.md`): about ×1.35 of our progression multiblocks per era, and power that is generous but never free.
- **Off-world resources only come from their world, and radioactives wait for IV.** `offworld_only.json` cuts on Earth, grouped by world:
  - titanium and helium-3 (the Moon);
  - tungsten, samarium and deuterium (Mars);
  - the radioactive leaks: thorium's uraninite, red granite's uranium, liquid ender air's radon, and the IV bee combs.
- **The EV validator** (`tiers/ev.json`, Moon and Mars substages) and the hidden table at EV. Every tier, steam to EV, passes on the release dump, and so does the obtain audit.
- **Release v0.6.0's integration:** every test passed (the main run with the client, plus the Moon, tripod, cryogenic plant and solar array runs).
  - It found two test bugs, both fixed: the old scheelite sample's check, and the book test's stale steam count.
  - It found one bench bug, also fixed: two servers fought over voice chat's UDP port.

  - The steam drill and greenhouse could run higher tiers' recipes; they now skip every tier folder.

- **AE2's Quantum Bridge Card** (AE2WTLib, already in the pack) lets a wireless terminal reach the home network from any dimension; a client test is proving it in our pack.

## What is broken or pending

- **Not seen in a real client yet:** EMI for EV's wave B and for IV. The gallery test covered names, models and controller faces; the release client run only checks that the client starts, loads the book and has no script errors.
- **Open risk:** fires outside GTM's recipes (vanilla furnaces, AE2's vibration chamber, Farmer's Delight stoves) still burn on the Moon. Vanilla furnaces on Mars are kept on purpose: the first merrillite is smelted in one.
- **Waiting for the user:** the boiler boost (keep it, x8 per tick with normal fuel use, or lower it). Until then it stays.
- **VM:** the user raised the VM's dynamic RAM from 8 to 14 GB after the crash. About five child sessions and a few background agents run at a time; free memory sits near 2 GB, so new work starts as old work ends.
