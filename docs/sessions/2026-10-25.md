## What works

- **Released today** (GitHub and the `jugable` channel, which is on **v0.6.1**):
  - **v0.5.2:** power closer to GregTech Leisure (x8 boilers, generator recipes twice as long), cable-free LV power (wireless connector, EU P2P), six cross-links between the LV–HV lines, the small and Large steam families, and the repository in English.
  - **v0.5.3:** Leisure conveniences: the LV generator array, the four MV universal factories, the advanced multi smelter, the lava furnace, the weather controller and the cleaning maintenance hatch.
  - **v0.5.4:** ten quests for those conveniences.
  - **v0.6.0, EV, the automatic Moon base and Mars:**
    - 11 multiblocks of ours: the platinum plant, extraction cascade, combined-cycle plant, hot strip mill, EV tripod drill, regolith cell, cryogenic plant, lunar solar array, deep space antenna, sealed dome and atmosphere processor;
    - the trip to Mars with its own veins;
    - fuels that only burn where there is air;
    - the Earth–Mars AE2 pair, and the wither;
    - an EV quest chapter of 66 quests.
  - **v0.6.1:** the gas collector collects air in the Overworld again. Our copies of its air recipe for the building dimensions used the same circuit, and GTM's recipe lookup keeps one recipe per set of inputs, so a copy hid GTM's recipe.
- **Every GT recipe is checked against GTM's own lookup** (`gg_test_recipe_lookup`, in every release run). Besides the air, it found 12 recipes that addons hide from each other. The ones that lose a route or run the wrong recipe (anthracene, the platinum sludge typo, bomb and dynamite, trinium carbonite) are being fixed; the harmless duplicates are accepted. Next, the validator will drop recipes the lookup can't reach.
- **Growth and power are pack rules** (rules 7 and 8 of `AGENTS.md`): about ×1.35 of our progression multiblocks per era, and power that is generous but never free.
- **Off-world resources only come from their world, and radioactives wait for IV.** `offworld_only.json` cuts on Earth, grouped by world:
  - titanium and helium-3 (the Moon);
  - tungsten, samarium and deuterium (Mars);
  - the radioactive leaks: thorium's uraninite, red granite's uranium, liquid ender air's radon, and the IV bee combs.
- **The EV validator** (`tiers/ev.json`, Moon and Mars substages) and the hidden table at EV. Every tier, steam to EV, passes on the release dump, and so does the obtain audit.
- **Release v0.6.0's integration:** every test passed (the main run with the client, plus the Moon, tripod, cryogenic plant and solar array runs).
  - It found two test bugs, both fixed: the old scheelite sample's check, and the book test's stale steam count.
  - It found one bench bug, also fixed: two servers fought over voice chat's UDP port.
- **Closed today:**
  - Earth's rain darkened the sky of the other planets (shared weather); `space/airless_weather.js` keeps them dry.
  - The steam drill and greenhouse could run higher tiers' recipes; they now skip every tier folder.
- **IV's design is raised to 15 multiblocks of ours** (`docs/eras/iv.md`, "Raising IV to the target"). The build packages are drafted in the session's scratch (`iv-build/PACKAGES-IV.md`).
- **IV, merged on the development branch** (not released), 14 of the era's 15 multiblocks of ours:
  - **wave A, complete:**

- **A crash of the app** at 17:15 interrupted two package sessions and a bench run. Nothing was lost: the uncommitted work was on disk, the sessions resumed, and the run was repeated.

## What is broken or pending

- **Not seen in a real client yet:** the textures, names and EMI of EV's wave B and of IV. The release client run only checks that the client starts, loads the book and has no script errors.
- **Open risk:** fires outside GTM's recipes (vanilla furnaces, AE2's vibration chamber, Farmer's Delight stoves) still burn on the Moon. Vanilla furnaces on Mars are kept on purpose: the first merrillite is smelted in one.
- **Waiting for the user:** the boiler boost (keep it, x8 per tick with normal fuel use, or lower it). Until then it stays.
- **VM:** the user raised the VM's dynamic RAM from 8 to 14 GB after the crash; four child sessions now run at a time.
