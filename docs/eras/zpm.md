# Era ZPM

ZPM is naquadria and the ringed and ice giants. LuV reached Jupiter's moons, put two stations in orbit and lit the first fusion ring. ZPM goes twice as far, to Saturn and Uranus, and **lands nowhere**:
- every base out there is a station in orbit;
- every sample of a moon or a deeper layer comes up by probe, siphon or flythrough.

At home, the impure naquadria solution stored since LuV becomes naquadria. Naquadria makes the second fusion ring and its fuel. The fuel burns in GTM's large naquadah reactor: the first power plant whose limit is a chemical line, not a generator count.

Out there, the giants are reservoirs:
- **Saturn** gives helium-3 and deuterium in bulk;
- **Titan's haze** gives tholins, an organic feedstock that exists nowhere else;
- **Enceladus's plume** gives salt water, silica nanograins and a living culture's food;
- **Uranus** gives the deepest gas and diamond slurry.

Cold is the second tool. Helium-3 runs the dilution refrigerator, and the dilution refrigerator runs qubits.

Power comes from:
- the large naquadah reactor on excited naquadah fuel, the era's main plant;
- the Mk2 on helium-3 fuels, through GTM's plasma turbine or our direct energy converter;
- everything older (fission, reactor III, Io, the tether), on the same wireless network.

LuV leaves, as built:
- the Mk1 (europium, the calcium plasma) and duranium's move to the Mk2 with a 200 M EU start;
- the research station, the HPCA and the ZPM energy hatch's data orb;
- the naquadah plant's mode 2 and the **impure naquadria solution, stored**;
- the HTS magnet works and the network's LuV tier;
- two stations (Earth orbit and Io orbit), the level 4 rocket and the cryogenic fuel tank;
- the bases on the belt, Io, Europa and Ganymede, with bacteria only from Europa and germanium only from Io;
- the coilgun's three hand-offs:
  - the Jovian atmosphere survey (`grindless:jovian_atmosphere_survey`), the gas giant siphon's calibration sample;
  - the Saturn transfer chart (`grindless:saturn_transfer_chart`), the ZPM trip;
  - the gravitational-wave catalogue (`grindless:gravitational_wave_catalogue`).

## Route

1. **ZPM grid.**
   - LuV's gate leaves the ZPM energy hatch and the ZPM components.
   - The ZPM assembler and mixer arrive as single blocks.
   - The **network's ZPM cells** are the era's first recipe: the naquadria magnet works (machine 6) winds URhNq₂ coils. Until every tower holds them, ZPM machines run on two LuV wireless energy hatches (GTM's two-hatch rule, proven in LuV).
2. **Naquadria.**
   - The naquadria refinery (1) turns the stored impure solution into naquadria.
   - Its enriched naquadah solution goes back to the naquadah plant.
   - Its gallium sulfide goes back to the MV gallium line.
3. **Europium without fusion.** GTCA's monazite froth line opens (IsaMill, flotation, thermal reactor). The europium reductor (3) takes the froth's rare-earth chlorides through the EV extraction cascade and isolates europium by valence.
4. **The Mk2.**
   - GTM's `gtceu:zpm_fusion_reactor`, built with the magnet works' coils.
   - Radon from the waste retrieval plant (4) and gallium give duranium (200 M start).
   - The plasma quench caster (7) turns duranium plasma straight into plate.
5. **Naquadah power.**
   - The naquadah fuel works (2) mixes the fuel from naquadria, cerium and neodymium.
   - The Mk2 excites it with radon; GTM's large naquadah reactor burns it.
   - The depleted fuel goes back to the fuel works.
6. **Level 5 rocket.** A naquadah rocket motor (KubeJS `gcyr:rocket_motor`, tier 5) and LuV's cryogenic tanks give level 5. The Saturn transfer chart is the motor recipe's catalyst.
7. **Saturn orbit, the third station.**
   - The station kit and Saturn's quantum seed (*Bridges*).
   - Then:
     - the ring harvester (water, silicates);
     - the gas giant siphon (calibrated with the Jovian survey);
     - the isotope column (helium-3, deuterium).
8. **Titan and Enceladus from Saturn orbit.**
   - The entry probe bay drops Titan probes, and the tholin reformer opens the organics.
   - The plume sampler flies probes through Enceladus's plume.
   - Their products go home:
     - the brine and organics to the vent bioreactor;
     - the silica to the aerogel works.
9. **Helium-3 at work.**
   - The Mk2 burns GTM's Ag + He-3 → tin plasma (280 M) and our D + He-3 → helium plasma (200 M, *tune*).
   - The direct energy converter (8) takes the plasma.
   - The dilution-fridge qubit fab (12) runs on ³He/⁴He and makes the qubit arrays that stand in for circuits in GTM's HPCA computation components: the era's research is paid in helium-3, not in ZPM and UV circuits.
10. **Level 6 rocket.**
    - The aerogel works' insulation gives the aerogel fuel tank (KubeJS `gcyr:fuel_tank`, tier 7): one naquadah motor (5) and aerogel tanks (7) make level 6.
    - The ring seismometer (24) writes the Uranus transfer chart, the tank recipe's catalyst.
11. **Uranus orbit, the fourth station.**
    - The ice-giant siphon goes deeper than Saturn's.
    - The slurry separator recovers diamonds.
    - The diamonds go home: the diamond lattice fab (10) and the ion implanter (11) make NV diamond and donor qubits.
12. **Neptune and the way out.**
    - The flyby probe bay (Uranus orbit) sends probes past Neptune and Triton.
    - The deep space network (16) receives them.
    - The Kuiper survey telescope (17, Earth orbit) finds the targets.
    - Together they write the Pluto–Kuiper chart, UV's trip.
13. **The neutrino observatory (15)** fills its tank once with IV heavy water and writes the solar-core survey, an input of the UV energy hatch's assembly-line recipe.

### 1. Naquadria refinery: `gtceu:naquadria_refinery`, type `gtceu:naquadria_refining` (Earth)

- **What it gains:** the impure naquadria solution has been stored since LuV (mode 2). GT--'s and GTM's path from it takes several machines: `gtceu:centrifuge/impure_naquadria_solution_separation`, then the sulfate steps, then `gtceu:distillation_tower/naquadria_waste_separation` (480 EU/t, 2,000 mB of waste → naquadria solution + enriched naquadah solution + gallium sulfide). Its loops leak unless the player closes each one. The refinery runs the whole chain in one run, and the waste's naquadria solution goes back inside.
- **Recipes** (composed from the pinned ids, as mode 2 was; S0-5 prints the balance):
  - c1: 1,000 mB impure naquadria solution + hydrogen and sulfuric acid charges → naquadria dust + enriched naquadah solution + gallium sulfide *(composed)*, 122,880 EU/t;
  - c2: 2,000 mB stored naquadria waste → the distillation's outputs, with the naquadria solution looped inside *(composed)*.
- **Byproducts:**
  - enriched naquadah solution → the naquadah plant's mode 3 (IV's machine);
  - gallium sulfide → the MV gallium line (roasted, then electrolysis);
  - sulfuric acid → back in the loop.
- **Structure:** a 7×7×11 tower of naquadria containment casing, with a centrifuge drum (2 layers) and a 5-high distillation stack. Tungstensteel pipe casing inside.
- **Controller:** ZPM hull + 2 ZPM pumps + ZPM sensor + 4 ZPM circuits *(tune)*.
- **Crossings:** IV (naquadah plant, mode 3), MV (gallium), LuV (mode 2's stored solution).
- **Risks:** GT--'s and GTM's naquadria ids must stay put. The pins fail loudly, as mode 2's did (S0-5).
- **Source:** GTM and GT-- (naquadria, from Stargate fiction); real solvent-extraction refining as the model.

## Decisions proposed (architect; for the orchestrator)

The design above follows these. None is in `DECISIONS.md` yet; the orchestrator accepts, changes or rejects each.

1. **All from orbit.** Saturn and Uranus are void orbits with a station each; no surface is reached or generated.
2. **Titan, Enceladus, Neptune and Triton are not registered.** They are probe targets: entry probes, a plume flythrough and a flyby, each a machine's product.
3. **The waste retrieval plant instead of a separate radon generator.** The hot cell keeps IV's radon; ZPM cleans the stored waste.
4. **Europium in ZPM through the reductor.** The Mk1's Nd + H stays; GTCA's froth stops at the mixed chlorides.
5. **Naquadria is the power cap,** and GT--'s large naquadah reactor is re-registered with duranium frames instead of neutronium (same id and research).
6. **The magnet works makes the URhNq₂ wire and the ZPM cell,** not fusion coils; GTM's fusion coil stays GTM's.
7. **Our D + He-3 → helium plasma** on the Mk2 (start 200 M), if S0-6 passes.
8. **The direct energy converter is written at its final figure** and is not in `power/generators.js`'s doubling list.
9. **Qubit arrays enter as alternative recipes for GTM's HPCA components;** GTM's `qbit_cpu_chip` and HPCA recipes stay.
10. **Proxima Centauri b moves to tier 8.**
11. **Two new stations** (Saturn orbit, Uranus orbit) through table-driven slots (F2); Neptune's station is UV's.
12. **The solar-core survey is a kept input of GTM's UV energy hatch,** re-registered with its research.
13. **The ZPM wireless row belongs to the package that makes its cell** (A5), as LuV-A4 did.
