# UIV (T11): neutron stars and a black hole

UIV arrives. No rocket goes this far: the nearest neutron star is about 400 light years out, and Cygnus X-1 is about 7,200. UEV's pulsar navigation chart (`docs/eras/uev.md:463`) fixes the way, and UHV's warp gate opens it. From the Sirius B relay, the gate throws a relay seed, a starchip with a sealed degenerate pellet and an exotic-matter cell, along a pulsar-timed course, and a far ring forms where it lands (named fiction, like UHV's gate). Three places come after it, and each gives something only it has:
- RX J1856.5−3754, the nearest isolated neutron star, has a crust that can be lifted: neutron-rich nuclei, drip neutrons and, contained, neutronium;
- PSR J0437−4715, the nearest millisecond pulsar, blows a wind of electron–positron pairs and keeps time well enough to navigate by;
- Cygnus X-1, the first black hole known, spins at almost its limit, and a Penrose engine takes energy from that spin.

At home, the era is metallic hydrogen, refractory high-entropy alloys, thin-film lithium niobate and a fifth fusion ring:
- a ramp-compression pulser squeezes solid hydrogen into metallic hydrogen, the UIV cable and coil, and makes lonsdaleite and polymeric nitrogen on the side;
- a levitation melter casts what no crucible holds: WMoNbTaHf alloys, Wf/RHEA first walls, titanium-50 for the element 120 beam, and bulk metallic glass gears;
- thin-film lithium niobate, femtosecond-written glass, 6.7 nm lithography, spectral holes in Eu:YSO, single phosphorus atoms placed by a scanning tunnelling microscope and a millikelvin cryostat are joined in UEV's photonic integration hall into GT--'s optical mainframe, the UIV circuit;
- the Mk5 ring burns polarised, neutron-free fuels (helium-3–helium-3, proton–lithium-6, proton–nitrogen-15) through ZPM's direct energy converter.

At Mercury, on UHV's station 7, a Matrioshka computer runs on the swarm's light and sheds its heat through liquid-tin droplet sheets. The network goes UIV on a pulsar-timed repeater.

### 1. Ramp-compression pulser: `gtceu:ramp_compression_pulser`, type `gtceu:ramp_compression` (Earth)

- **What it gains:** a shaped magnetic ramp that compresses a cold sample to hundreds of gigapascals without heating it as a shock would. UEV's megabar press holds static pressure in an anvil; it does not reach the metallic hydrogen transition on a scale.
  - real: Sandia's Z machine ramp-compressed liquid deuterium into a metallic fluid (Knudson 2015); shocked graphite gives lonsdaleite (Kraus 2016); cubic gauche nitrogen was made at 110 GPa and 2,000 K (Eremets 2004);
  - named fiction: the metastable quench of metallic hydrogen (predicted by Brovman 1972, never shown) and of cg-N to ambient pressure.
- **Recipes:**
  - c1: solid para-H₂ target → quenched metallic hydrogen rod (7,864,320 EU/t, 1,200 ticks *(tune)*);
  - c2: pyrolytic graphite → shocked lonsdaleite;
  - c3: laser-heated N₂ charge → cubic gauche nitrogen.
- **Byproducts:** H₂ shards and N₂ back to the EV cryogenic plant; graphite residue to stock *(kept)*.
- **Structure:** a cylinder 9 × 9 × 9 *(tune)* round a target chamber; the pulsed-power ring is the LuV coilgun launcher's capacitor casing (`luv/ganymede.js:136`); UEV casings elsewhere.
- **Crossings:** the LuV coilgun launcher (structure); lines 1, 3 and 4 reach the IV heavy water plant, the EV cryogenic plant, the UV superfluid helium plant and the ZPM methane pyrolyser.
- **Risks:** the quench is fiction; EMI and the quest say so.
- **Source:** Knudson 2015; Ashcroft 1968; Brovman 1972; Kraus 2016; Eremets 2004.

### 2. Levitation melter: `gtceu:levitation_melter`, type `gtceu:levitation_melting` (Earth)

- **What it gains:** containerless melting. A cold-crucible induction field holds a melt above 3,000 °C that every crucible would dissolve into, and undercools a melt far enough to make a bulk glass.
  - real: refractory high-entropy alloys (Senkov 2010); NASA's and JAXA's electrostatic levitators; Vitreloy 1 (Peker & Johnson 1993) and BMG strain-wave gears (Hofmann 2016, JPL).
- **Recipes:**
  - c1: W, Mo, Nb, Ta and Hf → WMoNbTaHf RHEA ingot (7,864,320 EU/t, 2,400 ticks *(tune)*);
  - c2: W fibre preform + RHEA melt → Wf/RHEA composite;
  - c3: Ti-50 sponge → Ti-50 metal, oven-charge grade;
  - c4: Zr–Ti–Cu–Ni–Be master alloy → undercooled melt, suction-cast → bulk metallic glass;
  - c5: Pr + Ni → PrNi₅.
- **Byproducts:** oxide dross to the EV extraction cascade *(kept)*; beryllium fume captured as BeO to stock *(contained, then kept)*.
- **Structure:** 7 × 7 × 9 *(tune)*, a cold-crucible coil of UEV superconductor, a vacuum hatch.
- **Crossings:** through lines 5–8: the EV extraction cascade, the EV hot strip mill, the IV fuel fabrication plant, the UV powder bed printer, the ZPM plasma quench caster.
- **Risks:** none in mechanism (plain recipe multiblock).
- **Source:** Senkov 2010; Peker & Johnson 1993; Hofmann 2016.

### 3. TFLN fab: `gtceu:tfln_fab`, type `gtceu:ion_slicing` (Earth)

- **What it gains:** heterogeneous bonding and poling. UHV's photonic foundry already slices LN onto silicon (`grindless:lnoi_wafer`, `photonics.json` foundry c1) and makes a thin-film LN modulator (c6). It cannot bond that film onto a different photonic platform, pole it, or split an isotopically pure silicon film.
  - real: heterogeneous LN-on-Si₃N₄ (Churaev 2023); electro-optically tuned soliton microcombs and lasers (Snigirev 2023); periodically poled thin-film LN (Wang 2018, Optica); Smart Cut SOI (Bruel 1995) on Si-28.

  - c4: annealed damascene Si₃N₄ ring + LNOI → EO-tunable soliton microcomb.

- **Structure:** a cleanroom 7 × 5 × 7 *(tune)* in UEV's cleanroom-grade photonic casing.

## Fusion, and what is not fusion

- **Mk5** is GTM's own fusion class, with `registerFusionTier(GTValues.UIV, ...)` (FusionReactorMachine.java:308-313), started from KubeJS startup as the Mk4 (DECISIONS.md:458; UIV-S0-6).
  - It gets its own pattern and casings, because GTM's switches stop at UV (:330-349). It needs UIV energy hatches (F1, UIV-S0-1).
  - Storage is hatches × 2^(tier − LuV) × 10,000,000 (:326-327). With 16 UIV hatches that is 5,120,000,000 EU, above the int maximum (UIV-S0-6).
  - The machine's voltage is the lower of its tier and its hatches (:276). A recipe whose `eu_to_start` is above storage is refused (:185, :294-298).
- **The recipes are in GTM's `gtceu:fusion_reactor` type:**
  - c1: `grindless:he3he3_fuel` → `grindless:he3he3_plasma` (eu_to_start about 4,000,000,000 *(tune)*);
  - c2: `grindless:pli6_fuel` → `grindless:pli6_plasma` (about 3,000,000,000 *(tune)*);
  - c3: `grindless:pn15_fuel` → `grindless:pn15_plasma` (about 4,800,000,000 *(tune)*, inside the 5.12 G buffer).

  The type takes fluids only, 2 in and 1 out (the Mk4 dropped its tritium side output for this, DECISIONS.md:489), so the fuels are pellet streams and each burns to a plasma of its own; the converter's three Mk5 rows give the EU and an ash gas, split as machine 10's *Byproducts* say. Premixed fuels and own plasmas keep our rows off GTM's and the Mk4's rows. UIV-S0-6 dumps the fusion rows to confirm there is no collision.
- **Why these fuels:** all three are aneutronic or nearly so, and all three need far hotter plasma than D–He-3 (p–N-15 is part of the CNO cycle). Polarisation raises the cross section (Kulsrud 1982). Their ash feeds lines 21, 25 and 27.
- **Not fusion:**
  - the pulsar wind trap and the Penrose engine;
  - the r-process decompressor (a nuclear process, not fusion; its energy goes to the decay vault);
  - the neutronium cage, which is a named fiction.
- **Neutrons:** p–Li-6 and p–N-15 make few; their side branches activate the first wall, which is the Wf/RHEA tile's wear *(tune)*.
